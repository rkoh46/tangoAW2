# BH dialogue: Sturm's scenes between missions (format: README.txt). Shown on the world map after a win, last of the scenes there (after the troops'
# talk and, at an act's end, the Accord's council): Sturm and the officers who have joined, on the result and on what comes next. They name only
# COs recruited before the mission they follow (M6/M7, M18/M19 and M24/M25 can be played in either order: no scene assumes its twin).

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- after M1 Storm Landing (Sturm, Von Bolt)

== m01_sturm
@SONG sturm
NARRATION: The ridge at dusk. Eleven green / tanks, and no paint to spare.
VON BOLT: Eleven tanks, mine and yours / now. Kehh... listen.
STURM: They are silent.
VON BOLT: The crystals, little storm. For / thirty years they hummed at nobody.
VON BOLT: Tonight they hum at the man / standing in the middle of them.
STURM: Then I will stand in the middle / of everything.
VON BOLT: And the Accord? It will notice a / ridge changing hands.
STURM: I want it to notice. A fence / that sees the storm builds nothing.
VON BOLT: Kehh-heh! Frightened men / overpay. I shall raise my prices.
