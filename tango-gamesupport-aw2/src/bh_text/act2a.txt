== m04_pre | STURM
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: Green Earth's northern march. / Fog, forest, and a Marshal.
NARRATION: Fort Verdant holds the only / road through the fog.
CRUMB(s): Sir, I can't see Sergeant / Mortar. He's right here.
MORTAR: Crumb. I am over here.
WICK(s): Is it all right if I say the / fog is eating the map?
STURM: Fog hides a march. It hides a / trap better. Report.
MORTAR: Empty roads, sir. Tidy ridges. / Too tidy.

HAWKE: That is rarer than you think. / Two columns before you did not.
STURM: I did not come for praise. I / came to take.
HAWKE: Of course not. Praise is for / people who need it.
HAWKE: Marshal Hawke, warden of the / northern march.
HAWKE: Green Earth holds the sea lanes / and the salvage rights.
HAWKE: I hold the fog that lets the / ministry sleep.
STURM: Wardens guard a fence. A fence / is a claim.
HAWKE: A neat line. I admire neat / lines. I draw them for a living.
VON BOLT: Kehh-heh! A Marshal! What does / he cost, Sturm?
HAWKE: Colonel Von Bolt. I have read / your rent notices.
HAWKE: Excellent penmanship. I paid / them from the ministry's purse.
VON BOLT: You paid ME? Kehh... then you / are a customer. Lovely.
HAWKE: I serve Green Earth. In name. / In green. Out of habit.
HAWKE: But I have waited a long time / for a player worth facing.
HAWKE: Show me. No wasted orders. No / wasted men.
HAWKE: The fog is mine. I like my odds.
CRUMB: Sir, is he allowed to say that / out loud?
HAWKE: Who is this?
CRUMB(h): Pip Hobb, sir! Runner! Crumb, / if you like.
HAWKE: A runner. How quaint.
HAWKE: Hobb. I will have your file / pulled. I like to know people.
CRUMB(h): I have a file?! Gerald, I / have a FILE!
HAWKE: Gerald?
CRUMB: My biscuit, sir. Basic training / issue.
HAWKE: ...Of course he is.

== m04_post | STURM
@SONG co:hawke
HAWKE(s): Enough. I concede the valley.
HAWKE: Before I go: why should I serve / you?
CRUMB: Sir, is the Marshal giving up? / He looks fine.
MORTAR: Crumb. He is a gentleman. That / is worse.
STURM: You will not kneel. You will / command under my banner.
STURM: Write the doctrine. I will / supply the storm.
HAWKE: A storm with no captain wrecks / only itself.
STURM: Then be the captain. Not mine. / The storm's.
HAWKE: ...Interesting. That is a / different offer.
HAWKE: Let me be exact. Green Earth / gave me a desk and a fence.
HAWKE: For thirty years I inspected a / border and called it duty.
HAWKE: The Accord froze everything in / place. I cannot stand that.
HAWKE: I wrote Black Hole's manual in / the margins of the audits.
CRUMB: The margins, sir? Of the audits?
HAWKE: Chapter one is finished. It is / called Patience.
VON BOLT: Marginal work! Kehh-heh! / Marshal, what is your pay?
VON BOLT: Every vault and port. You pick / the quarter. In writing.
HAWKE: You are selling me an empire / by the quarter.
VON BOLT: By the ton!
HAWKE: Crude. Effective. I like the / ledger.
HAWKE: I accept. But I will correct / your orders.
STURM: My orders are not corrected.
HAWKE: Then make them so good that I / have nothing to correct.
HAWKE: Every army needs a mind. I / volunteer mine.
STURM: There is a second folder under / your arm.
HAWKE: Chapter two. It is not / finished. It will keep.
STURM: What is in it?
HAWKE: Nothing you need today.
STURM: Noted.
HAWKE: I am loyal to the plan, Lord / Sturm. Always the plan.
STURM: It is my plan.
HAWKE: Of course. Until a better one / is drawn. Shall we begin?
HAWKE: A gift first. Skyhaven. Eagle's / aircraft sleep there.
HAWKE: Eight on the tarmac. Nine days. / By moonlight.
CRUMB: Does the Marshal need a runner, / sir?
HAWKE: ...Very well. You. Do not run / into me.
