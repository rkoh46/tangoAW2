# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M1 Storm Landing (Sturm fixed; enemy Von Bolt)

== m01_pre
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Cinder Coast at dawn. / The first storm in years.
NARRATION: Thirty years ago, this shore / was a war's front door.
CRUMB(h): Landing complete, sir! Lost / two boots. Only two!
MORTAR: Crumb. You packed spares.
CRUMB: Four pairs, Sergeant! And / Gerald. And soup.
STURM: Boots can be replaced. Report.
CRUMB: Green tanks on the ridge, green / flags on the walls.
CRUMB(s): Even the cook's apron was / green, sir. I checked.
MORTAR: Accord wardens, sir. Green / Earth sits on our ruins.
WICK(s): Is it all right if I ask? Are / they allowed to sit on ruins?
STURM: Thirty years ago, four nations / signed the Accord.
STURM: They fenced this continent. Then / they divided what was inside.
STURM: Crystal. Iron. Salvage. They / call it peace.
WICK: It sounds peaceful, sir.
STURM: A fence is not peace. A fence / is a claim.
STURM: I have come to answer it. One / nation at a time.
STURM: Look at the flags. New cloth / on old poles.
CRUMB: And the tanks are green all the / way down, sir.
WICK(s): I crept up to one, sir. It was / green under the green.
STURM: Wardens park their tanks. They / do not repaint them.
STURM: Someone bought this ridge. / Someone hides behind a flag.
VON BOLT: Kehh-heh! Who walks on my / beach without an appointment?
VON BOLT: A storm in a coat! So the / rumours were true. Kehh...
STURM: Von Bolt. You are older than / the rumours.
VON BOLT: Older than the Accord! When I / was young, the sea
VON BOLT: was a puddle. Kehh... I was old / even then. Remember that.
VON BOLT: Black Hole's last colonel! Its / whole army! Me.
VON BOLT: And richer than four nations' / pockets put together!
VON BOLT: They fenced my coast. So I / charge the fence rent.
VON BOLT: Every warden. Every digger. / Every salvage crew.
STURM: You sell Black Hole to the / nations that robbed it.
VON BOLT: I sell what I own! And I own / the ridge, the paint,
VON BOLT: the tanks! I bought a bankrupt / surplus dealer. Cheap!
VON BOLT: Green, but cheap. A colour is a / colour, little storm.
STURM: I will have what you guard. And / what you know.
VON BOLT: Everything I know is for sale. / Everything.
STURM: Then name your price.
VON BOLT: Beat me. Then we talk numbers. / Kehh-heh!
VON BOLT: And that boy! Those boots! / Genuine army leather!
CRUMB: Sir... may I keep the boots / I have left?
STURM: Fight in them.
STURM: Three crystals on the plain. / Hold them. The ridge falls after.
STURM: He has held this coast thirty / years. I take it today.

VON BOLT(s): ...No. It never did. The / number just sat there.

CRUMB(h): Does this mean I keep my / boots, sir?
VON BOLT: Hm. Perhaps. For now.
VON BOLT: Boot boy! Your name. I like to / know who owes me.
CRUMB: Pip Hobb, Colonel! But everyone / calls me Crumb.
VON BOLT: Hobb... Hobb. That name is in / a ledger. Somewhere.
VON BOLT: No matter. Old ledgers are full / of names. Kehh.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M2 The Sleeping Foundry (FREE, pool Sturm / Von Bolt; enemy Jess)

VON BOLT: Kehh... it wakes when the ledger / balances. Three days.
