# BH dialogue, act1: M1 Storm Landing, M2 The Sleeping Foundry, M3 Blockade Runner (format: README.txt)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M1 Storm Landing (Sturm fixed; enemy Von Bolt)

== m01_pre
NARRATION: Cinder Coast. Dawn. / The first storm in years.
NARRATION: Thirty years ago, this / shore was a war's front door.
CRUMB(h): Landing complete, sir! / Lost two boots. Only two!
MORTAR: Crumb. You packed spares.
CRUMB: Four pairs, Sergeant! / And Gerald. And soup.
STURM: Boots are replaceable. / Report.
CRUMB: Green tanks on the ridge, / green flags on the walls.
CRUMB(s): Even the cook's apron / was green, sir. I checked.
MORTAR: Accord wardens, sir. Green / Earth sits on our ruins.
WICK(s): Is it all right if I ask? / Is sitting on ruins allowed?
STURM: Thirty years ago, four / nations signed the Accord.
STURM: They fenced this continent. / Then they divided the contents.
STURM: Crystal. Iron. Salvage. / They call it peace.
WICK: It sounds peaceful, sir.
STURM: A fence is not peace. / A fence is a claim.
STURM: I have come to answer it. / One nation at a time.
STURM: Look at the flags. New / cloth on old poles.
CRUMB: And the tanks are green all / the way down, sir.
WICK(s): I crept up to one, sir. It / was green under the green.
STURM: Wardens park their tanks. / They do not repaint them.
STURM: Someone bought this ridge. / Someone hides behind a flag.
VON BOLT: Kehh-heh! Who walks / my beach unannounced?
VON BOLT: A storm in a coat! The / rumours were true. Kehh...
STURM: Von Bolt. You are older / than the rumours.
VON BOLT: Older than the Accord! / When I was young, the sea
VON BOLT: was a puddle. Kehh... I was / old even then. Remember that.
VON BOLT: Black Hole's last / colonel! Its whole army! Me.
VON BOLT: And richer than four / nations' pockets put together!
VON BOLT: They fenced my coast. / So I charge the fence rent.
VON BOLT: Every warden. Every / digger. Every salvage crew.
STURM: You sell Black Hole to the / nations that robbed it.
VON BOLT: I sell what I own! And / I own the ridge, the paint,
VON BOLT: the tanks! Bought a whole / bankrupt surplus dealer. Cheap!
VON BOLT: Green, but cheap. A / colour is a colour, little storm.
STURM: I will have what you / guard. And what you know.
VON BOLT: Everything I know is / for sale. Everything.
STURM: Then name your price.
VON BOLT: Defeat me. Then we / talk numbers. Kehh-heh!
VON BOLT: And that boy! Those / boots! Genuine army leather!
CRUMB: Sir... may I keep my / remaining boots?
STURM: Fight in them.
STURM: Three crystals on the plain. / Hold them. The ridge falls after.
STURM: He has kept this coast / thirty years. I take it today.

== m01_day3
VON BOLT: Those crystals! Mine! / They hum for ME!
STURM: They hum for whoever / holds them. I hold them.
VON BOLT: Kehh... no. They hum / in YOUR pitch. Strange.
STURM: Explain.
VON BOLT: ...Nothing. Old man's / ears. Forget I spoke.
STURM: Noted.

== m01_day7
VON BOLT: Kehh... send them all in. / It hurts me more than them!
VON BOLT: Every shell is a coin / thrown at the sky. Kehh...
VON BOLT: Charge! ...And collect / the brass casings after.

== m01_post
VON BOLT(s): My coast... my paint... / my lovely, cheap tanks...
VON BOLT: Thirty years I kept it. / Thirty years, nobody took it.
STURM: Nobody wanted it. I do.
VON BOLT: Kehh... then finish me. / Be quick. I am tired.
STURM: Von Bolt. You are not / finished. You are priced.
VON BOLT: Hm?
STURM: Four nations took your / continent and left you the rent.
STURM: You have counted it for / thirty years. It never grew.
VON BOLT(s): ...No. It never did. / The number just sat there.
VON BOLT: I count so I need not / count what years are left.
STURM: Join me. The world is a / ledger. I will write it.
STURM: Four columns. Four nations. / Every figure under one hand.
VON BOLT: The whole world... / in the black?
STURM: In mine. You may share it.
VON BOLT: Kehh... heh heh heh... / Gloriously greedy.
VON BOLT: Terms! First pick of / every vault we open.
STURM: You may pick second.
VON BOLT: ...Second pick. It / is still a pick.
VON BOLT: Done! Kehh-heh! You / drive a hard bargain.
STURM: I do not bargain. I allow.
VON BOLT: Allow! Even arrogance / has a price. I shall find yours.
STURM: You may try.
VON BOLT: Where do you come from, / storm? Nobody sails from nowhere.
STURM: From where the map ends.
VON BOLT: Hm. The old charts say / that about the Obelisk.
STURM: Then the charts and I / agree.
CRUMB(h): Does this mean I keep / my boots, sir?
VON BOLT: Hm. Perhaps. For now.
VON BOLT: Boot boy! Your name. I / like to know my debts.
CRUMB: Pip Hobb, Colonel! But / everybody says Crumb.
VON BOLT: Hobb... Hobb. That name / is in a ledger. Somewhere.
VON BOLT: No matter. Old ledgers / are full of names. Kehh.

== m01_map
CRUMB(h): Sergeant! The old man / says second pick is a pick!
MORTAR: Crumb. Second pick of / vaults. Not of boots.
CRUMB: Gerald says boots count.
MORTAR: Who is Gerald?
CRUMB(h): My biscuit, Sergeant! / Basic training issue.
MORTAR: That thing is older than / the Obelisk.
CRUMB: Gerald says that's rude.
WICK(s): Is it all right if I ask / why he has a little stamp?
WICK: Like a door. Or a keyhole. / It is... odd.
CRUMB: Maker's mark, Wick! Every / biscuit has one.
MORTAR: Mine does not.
CRUMB(s): ...Huh. I never thought / about that before.
WICK(s): Is... is anyone else worried / our tanks are still green?
VON BOLT: Paint costs money.
VON BOLT: And I do not spend it / on a coast I have lost. Kehh.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M2 The Sleeping Foundry (FREE, pool Sturm / Von Bolt; enemy Jess)

== m02_pre | STURM, VON BOLT
NARRATION: The Sleeping Foundry. / A hill of black iron and dust.
VON BOLT: Behold! The Foundry. / Asleep since the Accord.
VON BOLT: Thirty years of dust, and / not one warden dared knock.
STURM: It sleeps still.
VON BOLT: Kehh... it wakes when / the ledger balances. Three days.
STURM: Three days.
CRUMB: Sir, it's making a noise / like a cough.
VON BOLT: It is clearing its / throat. Kehh!
MORTAR: Crumb. Do not pet it.
CRUMB(s): I only said hello, / Sergeant. Gerald said hello too.
WICK(s): Is it all right if it / coughs at me? I did not agree.
VON BOLT: It coughs at everyone. / Equal treatment. Very fair.
JESS: Halt! Green Earth Coastal / Watch! Hands where I see them!
JESS: That Foundry is Accord / salvage. Nobody wakes it.
JESS: By order of Green Earth: / stand down. Last warning.
STURM: Your orders end here. This / soil is mine.
JESS: Orders say hold the line. / Command wants that Foundry shut.
JESS: So do I. Tanks first. / Paperwork after.
JESS: And I do not apologise for / the paperwork.
VON BOLT: Kehh! She threatens / politely. Delightful.
VON BOLT: Her tanks are the same / shade as mine. Cheap, too.
JESS: Green Earth Quartermaster / stock. Sold by a dealer.
JESS: A dealer who went bankrupt / very suddenly. Funny, that.
VON BOLT: Never met him. Bankrupt / men have such poor memories.
STURM: Enough. Hold the road and / the doors until it wakes.
STURM: Then let it answer for me.

== m02_pre_sturm | STURM
STURM: Hold the Foundry. Three / days. Then it speaks.
STURM: Whatever she sends, she / sends it into my storm.
VON BOLT: And what do I do, / little storm?
STURM: You count. It is what you / are for.
VON BOLT: Kehh-heh! My favourite / duty!

== m02_pre_other | VON BOLT
VON BOLT: Hold the Foundry for / three days. It will answer.
VON BOLT: Every shell she spends is / a coin I do not have to spend.
STURM: Do not lose what is mine.
VON BOLT: Mine, he says. I am only / renting the lease. Kehh...
STURM: Failure is a debt, Von / Bolt. I collect.

== m02_day3
NARRATION: The Foundry shudders, / coughs, and breathes fire.
CRUMB(h): It sneezed a Tank! / I'm calling him Dennis!
MORTAR: Crumb. It is a tank. / Not a stray dog.
CRUMB: Dennis says he is both, / Sergeant.
VON BOLT: Free tanks! Free! / Kehh-heh! Beautiful!
VON BOLT: Only the wiring cost / anything. Mark it down.
STURM: Hold your line.

== m02_day6
JESS: More tanks are coming. Hold / the line, Green Earth!
JESS: Foundry or no Foundry, they / bleed like everyone else.
VON BOLT: Kehh... she believes / in bleeding. How quaint.

== m02_post
JESS(s): Fall back to the coast. / Fall back!
JESS: Green Earth will hear of / this. We will return.
STURM: Return with more. The / Foundry is always hungry.
VON BOLT: She will tell everyone! / Free advertising! Kehh-heh!
STURM: Let them hear the storm / before it arrives.
JESS: Hear it? The wardens will / bring a navy, Black Hole.
JESS: Drake holds the Ember Strait. / Eagle holds the clouds above it.
JESS: You will not cross. You will / drown. I say that as a courtesy.
VON BOLT: Drown? On the sea? / Kehh... Sturm. Sturm. Sturm.
STURM: She is right that the / sea is next.
JESS: Brave, for a man with no / flag. Stupid, too. Good luck.
CRUMB: Sir? Dennis is fine. / Can he have a hat?
STURM: Fine. The tank still / answers to me.
CRUMB(h): Thank you, sir! He'll / wear it with honour!
MORTAR: Crumb. It is a tank.
CRUMB: Honour is not size-specific, / Sergeant.
WICK(s): Is it all right if I / say that was a good point?
MORTAR: ...Fine. It was a good / point.
VON BOLT: Kehh... the Foundry / has paid for itself. Nearly.
VON BOLT: Its next breath is / mine. Second pick. Remember.
STURM: Remember who owns / the Foundry.
VON BOLT: Kehh... a lease. / Only a long lease.

== m02_map
CRUMB(h): Dennis has a hat, Sergeant! / It's a bucket!
MORTAR: Crumb, that bucket is / Colonel Von Bolt's coin pail.
VON BOLT: My PAIL?!
CRUMB(s): Gerald, run.
VON BOLT: That pail has been / with me for fifty years!
VON BOLT: Do you know what I kept / in it? Dust and optimism!
CRUMB: Then Dennis will keep / them safe, Colonel!
VON BOLT: Kehh... boot boy... / return it. Rental begins now.
MORTAR: He's naming the tanks now.
MORTAR: Let him. It keeps him / out of the ammo.
WICK(s): Is it all right if I / name the other one?
MORTAR: Name it Not Dennis. / Then leave.
WICK(h): ...Thank you, Sergeant. / Not Dennis it is.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M3 Blockade Runner (Sturm + Von Bolt fixed pair; enemy Drake + Eagle)

== m03_pre
NARRATION: Ember Strait. Green / Earth's sea, patrolled and wired.
DRAKE: Ahoy, strangers! Welcome / to the Ember Strait!
DRAKE: Population: me, and a few / hundred ships. Great place.
EAGLE: Don't get comfy. Green / Earth owns sea and sky.
EAGLE: Wardens signed the Accord. / We keep the lanes. You pay.
EAGLE: Turn around, or I'll make / you walk home. On water.
STURM: We do not turn.
VON BOLT: Sturm, I am an old man. / I am seasick already.
STURM: Endure it.
DRAKE: Seasick? Heh, dude. A / pirate of the dry land?
VON BOLT: Pirate?! I am a / CREDITOR!
VON BOLT: Thirty years I paid you / a tenth of every crate!
DRAKE: Toll's the toll, old man. / Salvage rights. Lane upkeep.
DRAKE: Somebody has to scrub the / reefs. It's not cheap.
VON BOLT: Scrub the reefs! You / never scrubbed one reef!
EAGLE: Enough. The Accord does / not argue with salvage thieves.
EAGLE: You are not an army. You / are an invoice with legs.
VON BOLT: Kehh! Eagle, I have / never been so flattered!
STURM: Von Bolt. The sea.
VON BOLT: ...The sea. Yes. I am / hating it now, little storm.
STURM: The Strait is the lane / that feeds the Accord's fence.
STURM: Cut it, and every warden / on the coast starves of salvage.
STURM: Break the blockade. Their / sea will be mine by dusk.
DRAKE: Dude. Ambitious. Let's go, / Eagle. Show them how it's done.
EAGLE: Gladly. Stay out of my / sky, Drake. And my way.

== m03_day4
EAGLE: My Fighters own the / clouds! Dodge, sailors!
EAGLE: Keep your eyes up! The / ground is where you die.
VON BOLT: Kehh... boy. Hold my / hat. They are shooting at it.

== m03_day8
DRAKE: Not bad, dude. You've got / some wave in you after all.
EAGLE: Don't compliment them! / Sink them, Drake!
DRAKE: Dude, it's a good sea. Even / enemies deserve nice words.

== m03_post
DRAKE(s): ...I've been sunk more / gently by a bathtub.
EAGLE: Drake, pull out! Fall back / and regroup!
EAGLE: This isn't over. The / mainland has real pilots.
EAGLE: You think this was a war? / This was a warm-up lap.
STURM: The mainland is next.
VON BOLT: Gah... my stomach... no / more boats. Ever.
VON BOLT: I shall buy a mountain / and live on it. Kehh...
HAWKE: Admirable crossing. I have / been watching.
STURM: Hawke.
HAWKE: Marshal Hawke, officially. / Green Earth's marshal.
HAWKE: I have read your landing / and your Foundry. Interesting.
HAWKE: Rare, to meet a general who / treats the sea as a ledger.
VON BOLT: A Marshal! Of Green / Earth! On our radio! Kehh!
VON BOLT: Does he pay? Does he / owe? I shall inspect him.
HAWKE: I do not owe, Colonel. / I audit.
STURM: What do you want, Hawke.
HAWKE: A board worth commanding. / Fort Verdant has a road.
HAWKE: If you can walk it, do / come. I keep coffee for rivals.
STURM: I will walk it.
HAWKE: Splendid. Then I shall / see what you are made of.

== m03_map

MORTAR: The Colonel was sick in / a helmet. Not his.

MORTAR: The tall one doesn't know / how.
MORTAR: The old one's scared of / the sea. That counts.
STURM: Private.
CRUMB: Sir!
STURM: You carried your pack ashore / through three waves. Why.
CRUMB: Gerald is in it, sir. And / the soup. And the spare boots.
CRUMB: I did not want to leave / anything behind. Ever.
STURM: Black Hole does not leave / what is its own behind.
CRUMB(h): Yes, sir! I know, sir!
STURM: Noted.
VON BOLT: Sturm. A word. Alone. / Kehh... walk with an old man.
VON BOLT: When the war is done, come / to my vault. Last shelf.
VON BOLT: There is a crate I never / could price. First in my life.
STURM: Why not.
VON BOLT: Because every time I / pick it up, it hums. At you.
STURM: Then I will see it.
