HACHI: Last chance sale, friend! Barter! / Everything half price, everything goes!
EAGLE: Wing up! All of it! Let 'em look at / the sky!
LILI: Both Powers are lit. Watch the / cliff guns. They have the range.
LILI: The Salt Road column is pinned under / the cliff. Mr. Bear counts forty.
TABITHA: Then I take the field above them. / Hold the lane, Lili.
LILI: The repair lane is ours, Commander. / It will heal anything that reaches it.
TABITHA: Then reach it. Take the gate.

== m16_reserves
HACHI: Merchant Union, friend! Shelves / empty, everything out the door!
EAGLE: Reserves! The whole wing from the / lower field! Look up, Novarians!
LILI: Tanks on the road and a wing in the / air. Day six. The stores are open.
TABITHA: Then it is the last wave. Hold / the lanes. Then the till is empty.
LILI: A shop that empties its shelves has / nothing left to sell on day seven.
HACHI: Don't listen to her, Eagle. I / always keep something in the back.

== m16_epilogue
@SONG ending1
NARRATION: One year later. The harbour of / Port Argent.
NARRATION: Wheat, in the same sacks as / before. Different hands.
NARRATION: The corps are home. The frontier / is a customs line again.
LILI: Forty ships this month, Tabitha. / Every bill paid on time.
LILI: Every price written down. The / Directorate asked us both to stay.
TABITHA: It asked. We answered. The / Directorate is ours now.
LILI: I accepted. On terms. I am the first / quartermaster in history to
LILI: charge the Treasury interest. Tee / hee. Mr. Bear gets a wage.
LILI: Hachi's invoice for the glass at / Stormhold arrived. I paid it last.
LILI: Tabitha. Look at this wagon. Eleven / days from the north.
LILI: Eleven. I said twelve. I was wrong. / The green one is delighted.
LILI: The channels are cut. The southern / fields are green again.
LILI: The north is still grey. The ash / does not leave quickly.
TABITHA: It will. I will see to it. Give it / a decade.
LILI: I have a decade. I have nothing but / decades and toys.
TABITHA: Father is a cell number. His / empire is our workshop. His war is ours.
TABITHA: I came to the sea because the / bread stopped. Now it has started.
TABITHA: I took a great deal on the way. I / will take the rest. For us.
TABITHA: We fed people. That is a start. / The weak will eat what we allow.
LILI(s): You sound as if you are saying / goodbye, Tabitha. I do not like it.
TABITHA: I am not. Stupid weak people / leave. I stay.
TABITHA: And you, Lili, will stay beside / me. Where I can see you.
LILI: Tee hee. Always. Tabitha. The last / crate.
LILI: From the northern shipment. It is / not on my manifest.
LILI: What is that on the lid? A mark. / Black. A stencil.
LILI: It is the stencil. It is paid in / advance, Tabitha. All of it.
TABITHA: Close the lid.
TABITHA: No one opens that crate. No one / eats from it. Not yet.
TABITHA: Lili. Keep the bill.
LILI: I always keep the bill.
NARRATION: Dusk. The harbour lamps are lit / one by one.
LILI: Tabitha. What is that, on the / northern horizon?
NARRATION: A light. Black, if light can be / black. It does not flicker.
