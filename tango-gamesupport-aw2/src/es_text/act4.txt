# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M13 The Free Port (four armies, no alliances)

@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Free Port of Calderon. / Midsummer, a hot grey haze.
NARRATION: Scorched wasteland all around. The / bays glow like lava.
NARRATION: No nation owns the bay. Every / nation owns a warehouse.
NARRATION: Reefs break the glowing bays. The / rivers run red, not blue.
AURELIA: Four flags on one harbour. I've / never seen a worse plan.
LIVIA: Four flags and, by my count, eleven / creditors, Chief.
LIVIA: The port charges a berth fee per / flag. We have not raised one.
LIVIA: I am told that is what makes it / free. The fee is for later.
CASSIUS(h): A joke! Livia made a joke! / Somebody write down the date!
LIVIA: It was a statement of terms, / Prefect. The date is on the bill.
KLAUS: Report, Prefect.
CASSIUS: Green Earth holds the west, past / the Rio Sale. Javier's banners, and
CASSIUS: a lot of towers he built in a / week. The man swears oaths at bricks.
AURELIA: He builds fast and he builds thin. / I like him already.
CASSIUS: Yellow Comet took the east, by / the Rio Dolce, before dawn. Sonja.
CASSIUS: Nobody saw them land. The / sentries woke up in a ditch.
KLAUS: And the north-east dock.
CASSIUS(s): Black Hole. The real thing, old / man. Not a stray banner.
CASSIUS: Walls, cranes, the Black Factory / and a proper garrison. Kindle.
LIVIA: The trail ends there, Legate. / Every barge we have chased
LIVIA: was loaded at the north dock. The / yard keeps its own books.
KLAUS: Black Hole is not at war with / Aurea. Remember that.
AURELIA: Somebody should tell their guns. / They haven't noticed.
KLAUS: They defend cargo. They take us for / raiders. They are half right.
KLAUS: We came for a ledger, not a fight. / Today we may get both.
CASSIUS: Where's the ledger? Which / warehouse? Which crate?
LIVIA: The Harbour Office, in the middle / of the octagonal square. The harbour
LIVIA: master files every bill of lading. / The stencil will be there.
KLAUS: Then the Harbour Office is the / objective. Take it.
KLAUS: Calderon calls itself free. Today / it answers to the road.
KLAUS: Four provinces behind us. This / port is the gate to the northern coast.
KLAUS: And hold it for three days. A file / is not read in an hour.
AURELIA: Three days in the middle of four / armies. In an octagon. Lovely.
AURELIA: Everyone will shell the one roof. / I'll bring stone anyway.
AURELIA: Kindle's quarter is north. The / bays glow: no ship can sail them.
AURELIA: Every quay has a factory or an / airfield instead. Hers too.
AURELIA: Tanks, Md Tanks and Artillery / answer her armour. The AA, her wing.
AURELIA: The Black Factory feeds her. / Bombers answer it, over the reefs.
KLAUS: There are no alliances in the / port. Every flag fights every flag.
KLAUS: That is also our chance. Let them / spend themselves on each other.
LIVIA: I have noted who owes whom. Javier / owes Sonja. Sonja owes no one.
LIVIA: Kindle owes everyone, and collects / from everyone besides.
CASSIUS: So we hit whoever gets close and / let the rest bleed? Easy!
KLAUS: Not easy. Patient. You will learn / it in a week, Prefect.
CASSIUS: A week? I'll learn it by noon, and / be bored by one!
KLAUS: Then be bored carefully.
KLAUS: Quaestor, count what we have. / Prefect, ready the horses.
KLAUS: Engineer, find me a roof that / holds. We move at first light.
AURELIA(h): Aye, Legate. I always wanted a / view of a siege.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M14 Varro's Gambit (the storming of Castra Maxima; Varro falls)

NARRATION: Walls, a moat river, a harbour and / three roads in. All guarded.

LIVIA: "The Senate recalls Legate Klaus to / answer for the north."
LIVIA: "The legions pass to the Consul. / Prefect Cassius will report in."

KLAUS: I can read the difference, / Prefect.
CASSIUS: And I'm ordered to the camp. By the / same hand. With my horses.
KLAUS: You are. And you are free to go. I / do not hold a man by the collar.
CASSIUS(s): ...You'd let me ride to him? / Today? After everything?
KLAUS: You carried his letter for a year. / You owe him one answer.
LIVIA: Prefect. Before you go, take this. / One page, sealed, with my witness mark.
CASSIUS: A page. What's on it?
LIVIA: A line from the Free Port book. The / war chest, advanced to the Consul.
LIVIA: Nine hundred talents. Every coin / stamped with the mark on the crates.
LIVIA: Read it alone. I would rather you / read than believe.
CASSIUS: You're a terrible comfort, Livia.
LIVIA: I am an accurate one. That is / rarer, and cheaper.
CASSIUS: ...I'll read it at the camp. Don't / wait up, old man.
KLAUS: I will not. I will be at the gate. / With a ladder.
VARRO: Klaus, my boy. I hoped you would / come quietly. You never did.
KLAUS: Consul. You built a city on the / coast, for a letter.
VARRO: I built a camp, dear boy, so that / the conquest is finished properly.
VARRO: Four nations shaken, an Accord / frightened. And you stop to ask why.
VARRO: Aurea will not starve while I am / Consul. Come home. All is forgiven.
KLAUS: Someone feeds Aurea on purpose, / Consul. I have the book.
VARRO: A clerk's book, in a clerk's hand. / Burn it, and be forgiven.
VARRO: I found you money when no one else / would. I asked obedience. Nothing more.
KLAUS: You never asked where the money / came from, either.
VARRO: A father does not ask where the bread / comes from, my boy. He gives thanks.
LIVIA: I have audited that bread, Consul. / The thanks are overdue.
VARRO: Quaestor Livia. A brilliant girl. / Your debts are forgiven too.
LIVIA: I did not ask to be forgiven. I / asked a question. The terms differ.
VARRO: Courage in a clerk. How rare. Do / mind the ridge track, my boy.
VARRO: My Praetorians have never lost a / wall. It would be a pity to teach them.
KLAUS: Then we will teach them, Consul. / Politely.
AURELIA: The plan. Three lanes, one camp. / Heavy guns take the coast road.
AURELIA: Rockets and engineers up the / valley. Light units and Copters, ridge.
AURELIA: Warning: reserves land on day five, / and again on day eight.
AURELIA: If we're not through the walls by / the fifth, we fight a second army.
KLAUS: Then we do not wait for them. We / have taken a coast, a pass, a port.
KLAUS: We will take this camp, and the man in it. / Quaestor, funds. Engineer, ladders.
LEGIONARY: The legion is formed, Legate. All / three lanes.
KLAUS: Then storm it.
