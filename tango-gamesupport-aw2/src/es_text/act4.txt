# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M13 The Free Port (four armies, no alliances)

== m13_pre
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Free Port of Calderon. / Midsummer, a hot grey haze.
NARRATION: No nation owns the bay. / Every nation owns a warehouse.
AURELIA: Four flags on one harbour. / I have never seen a worse plan.
LIVIA: Four flags and, by my count, / eleven creditors, Prefect.
LIVIA: The port charges a berth fee / per flag. We have not raised one.
LIVIA: I am told that is what makes / it free. The fee is for later.
CASSIUS(h): A joke! Livia made a joke! / Somebody write down the date!
LIVIA: It was a statement of terms, / Prefect. The date is in the bill.
KLAUS: Report, Prefect.
CASSIUS: Green Earth holds the west / quay. Javier's banners, and a lot
CASSIUS: of towers he built in a week. / The man swears oaths at bricks.
AURELIA: He builds fast and he builds / thin. I like him already.
CASSIUS: Yellow Comet took the east / mole before dawn. Sonja's work.
CASSIUS: Nobody saw them land. The / sentries woke up in a ditch.
KLAUS: And the north dock.
CASSIUS(s): Black Hole. Real ones, old / Legate. Not a stray banner.
CASSIUS: Walls, cranes, a signal tower / and a proper garrison. Kindle.
LIVIA: The trail ends there, Legate. / Every barge we have chased
LIVIA: was loaded at the north dock. / The yard keeps its own books.
KLAUS: Black Hole is not at war / with Aurea. Remember that.
AURELIA: Somebody should tell their / guns. They have not noticed.
KLAUS: They defend cargo. They take / us for raiders. They are half right.
KLAUS: We came for a ledger, not a / fight. Today we may get both.
CASSIUS: Where is the ledger? / Which warehouse? Which crate?
LIVIA: The Harbour Office, in the / middle of the port. The harbour
LIVIA: master files every bill of / lading. The stencil will be there.
KLAUS: Then the Harbour Office is / the objective. Take it.
KLAUS: And hold it for three days. / A file is not read in an hour.
AURELIA: Three days in the middle of / four armies. In a bowl. Lovely.
AURELIA: Everyone will shell the one / roof. I will bring stone anyway.
KLAUS: There are no alliances in the / port. Every flag fights every flag.
KLAUS: That is also our chance. Let / them spend themselves on each other.
LIVIA: I have noted who owes whom. / Javier owes Sonja. Sonja owes no one.
LIVIA: Kindle owes everyone, and / collects from everyone besides.
CASSIUS: So we hit whoever gets close / and let the rest bleed? Easy!
KLAUS: Not easy. Patient. You will / learn it in a week, Prefect.
CASSIUS: A week? I will learn it by / noon, and be bored by one!
KLAUS: Then be bored carefully.
KLAUS: Quaestor, count what we have. / Prefect, ready the horses.
KLAUS: Engineer, find me a roof / that holds. We move at first light.
AURELIA(h): Aye, Legate. I always / wanted a view of a siege.

NARRATION: Day three. The Harbour / Office is quiet, and ours.
AURELIA: Roof held. Two shells and a / lot of dust. Stone does not lie.
CASSIUS: Javier retreated to his / towers. Sonja simply vanished.
CASSIUS: Kindle is still in the north / dock. She waves. I hate her.
LIVIA: Legate. The master's strong / room. It was not even locked.
LIVIA: A single book on the table. / Black cloth. Bills of lading.
KLAUS: Read it aloud, Quaestor.
LIVIA: Grain. Three harvests of it, / ship by ship. Paid in advance.
LIVIA: Every entry carries the same / stencil in the margin. And a second
LIVIA: column the harvest never / mentioned. Jars. Sealed, grey.
LIVIA: Delivered to the northern / granaries on the same dates.
KLAUS: Grain in one hold, ash in / the next. Same barge, same bill.
AURELIA: You do not feed a country / and poison its fields by accident.
KLAUS: No. Someone is feeding Aurea / on purpose. I said it. Now it is on paper.
CASSIUS(s): Then my letter was right / about one thing. The grain is up north.
CASSIUS: Just not where I was told / to take it from. Who wrote that book?
KLAUS: Quaestor, the hand.
LIVIA: A clerk's hand. Small, even, / very old school. Not a Senate hand.
KLAUS: I know the Consul's writing. / I have had forty years of letters.
KLAUS: This is not Varro's. The / approvals bear his name. Not his pen.
AURELIA: Someone signs for the / Consul? Or the Consul signs for them?
KLAUS: I do not know. I intend to ask.
LIVIA: Legate. The stencil.
LIVIA: I have seen this mark before. / On a bill. I will say no more.
KLAUS: You do not need to. Not yet.
LIVIA: I thank you. When I know, you / will be the first, in writing.
KINDLE: Cough. Excuse me. Radio. I / do hope I am not interrupting.
KINDLE: You have the Harbour Office. / Congratulations. Keep the book.
KINDLE: The crates, the cranes and / the north dock stay mine. Paid for.
KLAUS: By whom, Kindle?
KINDLE: By someone who pays in / advance. I never ask for names. Names
KINDLE: are for the unpaid. Go home, / green soldier. You frighten my dock.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M14 Varro's Gambit (hold the quay, then break out)

== m14_pre
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: Port Quirinal, the Aurean / garrison town on Green Earth's coast.
NARRATION: Sails off the point at / dawn. Hundreds. All of them red.
LEGIONARY: Legate! A fleet, forty / hulls at least. Senate colours!
LEGIONARY: Praetorian banners on the / lead ships. And a herald's boat.
CASSIUS(s): The Praetorians. Varro's / own guard. They never leave Aurelium.
AURELIA: They have left now. With / marines, siege rams and a barge
AURELIA: of ladders. They did not / pack that for a polite chat.
LIVIA: The herald carries a letter / for the Legate. The Consul's ring.
KLAUS: Read it.
LIVIA: The Senate recalls Legate / Klaus to Aurelium to answer for
LIVIA: his failure to take the north. / The legions pass to the Consul.
LIVIA: Signed Varro, with the full / Senate's seal beneath. All of it.
CASSIUS: Recalls. It is an arrest, / old man. Politely written.
KLAUS: I can read the difference, / Prefect.
VARRO: Klaus, my boy. I hoped the / herald would arrive before I did.
KLAUS: Consul. You crossed the sea / for a letter.
VARRO: I crossed the sea for you. / Do not scowl. It ages a man.
VARRO: You have done splendid work. / Three nations beaten, an Accord
VARRO: shaken. But the road ends. / The north will be taken, with or
VARRO: without your scruples. Aurea / will not starve while I am Consul.
KLAUS: Someone feeds Aurea on purpose, / Consul. I have the ledger.
VARRO: A ledger. How good. In whose / hand, my boy?
KLAUS: Not yours.
VARRO: Then it is a clerk's fancy. / Burn it, and come home. All is
VARRO: forgiven. I found you money / when no one else would. I never
VARRO: asked for gratitude. I ask / for obedience. It is a smaller thing.
KLAUS: You never asked where the / money came from either.
VARRO: A father does not ask where / the bread comes from, my boy. He
VARRO: gives thanks and eats. You / should try it. Fewer nightmares.
LIVIA: I have kept the Consul's / accounts for six years. Never once
LIVIA: has the Senate's treasury / explained a gift. I should like to.
VARRO: Quaestor Livia. Brilliant / girl. Your debts are forgiven too.
LIVIA: I did not ask to be forgiven. / I asked a question. Terms differ.
LIVIA: He forgave a debt I never / owed. I shall invoice him for it.
VARRO: And Prefect Cassius. You still / carry my letter, do you not?
CASSIUS: I... what letter would that / be, Consul? I have many.
VARRO: The one that says what you / must do if the old man falters.
VARRO: He has faltered. Bring him in. / You will be Consul-elect by spring.
CASSIUS(s): Consul-elect. Hear that, / Klaus? He has a whole speech ready.
KLAUS: What will you do, Cassius?
CASSIUS: I do not know yet, / damn it. Give me until I do.
VARRO: A sensible young man. Take / your time. Take until nightfall.
VARRO: The Praetorians land at / noon. Hold your quay, if you can.
KLAUS: Engineer. Plan.
AURELIA: The quay is a bottle with / one neck. Hold the HQ six days
AURELIA: until the transports are / ready to leave. Then fight out to
AURELIA: the docks. Two of them. / That is the neck, and I will build it.
KLAUS: Six days. Then we leave.
KLAUS: Quaestor, give me their numbers. / Prefect, ride the east gate.
CASSIUS: The east gate. Right. / Where I am useful and out of sight.
KLAUS: Where you can think. Go.

== m14_day4

CASSIUS: it says, and I know what you / meant by it. And I am ashamed.

KLAUS: Welcome back, Prefect.
CASSIUS: Do not be nice. I will cry / and the horses will see.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M15 Pax Romana (the Strait of Elms)

== m15_pre
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Strait of Elms. Two / islands, one narrow, deep channel.
NARRATION: Varro's navy sits at the / far end. His air wing circles.
AURELIA: Battleships at the mouth. / Cruisers on the flanks. Carriers.
AURELIA: And the wing is Bombers and / Fighters. All of it overhead.
CASSIUS: He emptied Aurelium into / this strait. Every ship we own.
LIVIA: Every ship the Senate owns. / I have a list. The list is long.
KLAUS: What do we have?
LIVIA: Cruisers, a Battleship, two / Subs and a dozen transports,
LIVIA: Legate. Fuel for six days. / And an Accord that has not replied.
KLAUS: Then we use what we have. / This is a pair's fight.
KLAUS: Tag Power on the screen. / Anti-air and Cruisers first.
KLAUS: Fire both Powers and bring the / line through together. Together.
CASSIUS: Pax Romana! Say it with / me, Quaestor. It rolls off the tongue.
LIVIA: I do not roll, Prefect. I / disburse. The Power is a good price.
AURELIA: Grandiose name, honest / numbers. Two stars. Let us spend it.
VARRO: Klaus, my boy. You are late. / I have waited since dawn.
KLAUS: Consul. You commanded the / sea. You may lose it.
VARRO: I command nothing, dear boy. I / merely supply. The Senate commands.
VARRO: Pull back to Elms, surrender / the fleet, and I will see that
VARRO: the grain flows north again. / No one dies. Not a legionary.
KLAUS: No one dies. And the grain / flows. From whose hand, Consul?
VARRO: From the hand that has always / fed you. You know better than to ask.
LIVIA: I know exactly what to ask. / The fee for that hand is the war.
VARRO: Quaestor. Clever, clever. / Always counting what is not yours.
NELL: Legate Klaus. This is Nell, / on the Accord's channel. Can you hear?
KLAUS: I hear you, Nell.
NELL: The Accord has voted. Orange / Star, Green Earth, Blue Moon and
NELL: Yellow Comet are all sailing. / We reach you on the sixth day.
NELL: Hold the straits that long, / and you will not hold them alone.
EAGLE: Death from above! Hey, / Legate, wait for the wing, huh?
DRAKE: Dude. We are coming. The seas / will be fine. Just breathe.
VARRO: Ah. The Accord. Always late, / always sure it is invited.
VARRO: Hold the straits six days, / then. Admiral, release the wing.
KLAUS: Six days. Quaestor, what do / we sell to buy them?
LIVIA: Time, Legate. It is the only / currency in short supply.
KLAUS: Then spend it well.
