CASSIUS: of towers he built in a week. / The man swears oaths at bricks.

LIVIA: was loaded at the north dock. / The yard keeps its own books.

KLAUS: They defend cargo. They take / us for raiders. They are half right.
KLAUS: We came for a ledger, not a / fight. Today we may get both.

LIVIA: master files every bill of / lading. The stencil will be there.

KLAUS: Calderon calls itself free. / Today it answers to the road.
KLAUS: Four provinces behind us. This / port is the gate of the northern coast.
KLAUS: And hold it for three days. / A file is not read in an hour.

AURELIA: Everyone will shell the one / roof. I will bring stone anyway.

LIVIA: Kindle owes everyone, and / collects from everyone besides.
CASSIUS: So we hit whoever gets close / and let the rest bleed? Easy!
KLAUS: Not easy. Patient. You will / learn it in a week, Prefect.
CASSIUS: A week? I will learn it by / noon, and be bored by one!
KLAUS: Then be bored carefully.
KLAUS: Quaestor, count what we have. / Prefect, ready the horses.
KLAUS: Engineer, find me a roof / that holds. We move at first light.
AURELIA(h): Aye, Legate. I always / wanted a view of a siege.

LIVIA: I am an accurate one. It is / rarer, and cheaper.
CASSIUS: ...I will read it at the camp. / Do not wait up, old man.

KLAUS: Consul. You built a city on the / coast, for a letter.

VARRO: Aurea will not starve while I / am Consul. Come home. All is forgiven.

VARRO: I found you money when no one else / would. I asked obedience. Nothing more.
KLAUS: You never asked where the / money came from, either.

LIVIA: I have audited that bread, / Consul. The thanks are overdue.
VARRO: Quaestor Livia. Brilliant girl. / Your debts are forgiven too.
LIVIA: I did not ask to be forgiven. / I asked a question. Terms differ.
VARRO: Courage in a clerk. How rare. / Do mind the ridge track, my boy.
VARRO: My Praetorians have never lost / a wall. It would be a pity to teach them.
KLAUS: Then we will teach them, / Consul. Politely.
AURELIA: Plan. Three lanes, one camp. / Heavy guns take the coast road.
AURELIA: Rockets and engineers up the valley. / Light units and Copters on the ridge.
AURELIA: Warning: reserves land on day / five and again on day eight.
AURELIA: If we are not through the walls by / the fifth, we fight a second army.

KLAUS: We will take this camp, and the man in it. / Quaestor, funds. Engineer, ladders.
LEGIONARY: The legion is formed, / Legate. All three lanes.
KLAUS: Then storm it.
