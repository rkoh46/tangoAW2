# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M13 The Free Port (four armies, no alliances)

== m13_pre
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Free Port of Calderon. / Midsummer, a hot grey haze.
NARRATION: No nation owns the bay. Every / nation owns a warehouse.
LILI: Four flags on one harbour, Sister. / The port charges a berth fee per flag.
LILI: We have not paid one yet. They say / that is why it is free: it comes later.
TABITHA: Report. Who holds what? Briefly.
LILI: Green Earth, west, past the Rio Sale. / Javier. Towers built in a week.
LILI: Yellow Comet, east, by the Rio / Dolce. Sonja. Nobody saw them land.
LILI(s): And Black Hole, north-east dock. / Kindle, walls, the Black Factory.
LILI: Every barge we chased was loaded / there. The trail ends at that dock.
TABITHA: Black Hole is not at war with / me. Yet. They take us for raiders.
TABITHA: I came for a ledger. Where is / it kept?
LILI: The Harbour Office, mid-square. / The harbour master files every bill.
TABITHA: Then that is the objective. Take / it, and hold it for three days.
TABITHA: A file is not read in an hour. / Calderon answers to me today.
LILI: Three days, four armies, one roof. / Everybody will shell it.
LILI: The bays glow, so no ship can sail. / Every quay is a factory or an airfield.
LILI: Kindle's Black Factory feeds her. / Bombers answer it, over the reefs.
TABITHA: No alliances here. Every flag / fights every flag. Let them bleed.
TABITHA: We hit whoever comes close. / Count what we have. We move at dawn.
LILI(h): Yes, Sister! Mr. Bear has packed / the popcorn. Hee hee.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M14 Caulder's Gambit (the storming of Fort Meridian; Caulder falls)

LILI: Nine thousand soldiers landed in / eleven days. They walled a coast.
LILI: Three lanes. Coast road to the south / gate: heavy guns. Valley road, east:

@WITH COLIN

TABITHA: Take the gate, Colin, or lose it. / And your head.
@END
LILI: Daddy's herald came at midnight. / "The corps pass to the Director."
LILI: "Commander Tabitha is recalled to / answer for the north. Lili reports in."
TABITHA: Father calls it a recall. It is / a leash. I do not wear leashes.
LILI(s): I am ordered to the fort. With / my detachment. After the needles...
LILI: I copied the war chest page. I will / read it in his camp. Mr. Bear listens.
CAULDER: Tabitha. Lili. I hoped you would / come quietly. You never did.
CAULDER: I built a camp so the conquest ends / properly. Come home. I will overlook this.
LILI: I looked at the money, Daddy. It / has a stamp. A mean one.
CAULDER: Lili. How tedious. After all I put / into you.
LILI: I remember every needle, Daddy.
CAULDER: Do mind the ridge track, girls. / I am curious what will break.
CAULDER: My Directorate Guard has never / lost a wall. It would be a pity to teach it.
TABITHA: Then I will teach it, Father. We / do not wait for the reserves.
TABITHA: I will be at the gate, with a ladder. / Storm it. Leave the Guard nothing.

== m14_day4 | KANBEI, COLIN
