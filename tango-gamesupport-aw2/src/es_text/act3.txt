# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M9 Grain Ships (Black Hole freight yards: Adder, Flak)

== m09_pre
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: Kite Hill, on the northern / coast. Ten days until the tide.
LILI: Three barges in the east bay! / They sail on day eleven.
LILI: The bill of lading says grain. The / stamp is the usual one.
TABITHA: Then the barges are the road. / Sink them before they sail.
LILI: The bay glows red, Sister. Mr. Bear / says lava. I say soup.
LILI: Kite Hill holds the shore: a / plateau, a depot, four bridges.
TABITHA: Take the depot and the northern / shore falls with it.
NARRATION: Kite Hill is scorched wasteland. / Ash, cinders, ruins, rough rock.
LILI: Cinders and ruins give cover. But / the air is clear. Wings see far.
TABITHA: Then our wing sees every ant on / that plateau. Use it.
LILI: Look at the ridge. Missiles, and / flak tanks at the gate. Ooh, scary.
TABITHA: The ground columns crush the / ridge. The wing follows.
LILI: A pipeline walls off the freight / yard. It has one seam. Pop!
LILI: A Piperunner guards it. Fast, / armoured, slides on pipes.
TABITHA: Break the seam early, before a / second one rolls out.
LILI: Their base by the pipe builds / Rockets, Tanks and Bombers.
TABITHA: Black Hole's quay lies east. It / sells to anyone. Move out.

== m09_pre2
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The freight yards. The / quartermasters' radio channel.
ADDER: A silver star on a grey field. / How very fresh. Quartermaster Adder.
FLAK: Raiders, Adder? Flak likes / raiders. Raiders bring fuel.
ADDER: They are not raiders until they / have raided, Flak.
TABITHA: Commander Tabitha of Novaria. / Kneel, and I may ignore you.
ADDER: Delightful! Then why the bombers?
TABITHA: Your barges carry a stencil I / followed across an ocean.
ADDER: Ah, the stencil. A lovely account. / It pays well, and early.
FLAK: Flak guards Adder. And the / sandwiches. Both in danger.
LILI: Name the buyer and I pay double! / Mr. Bear agrees.
ADDER: Madam, I would sell my own hat / for double. I do not know him.
ADDER: Nobody does. The crates simply / arrive. The coin simply clears.
TABITHA: Then I will read the crates / myself. Over your wreckage.
ADDER: Read them from the lake! Fighters / up!
FLAK: Up, Adder? Up is for the planes.
ADDER: Metaphorically, Flak. Fire.

== m09_day4

LILI: The barges lit their lamps, Sister. / All three. Pretty.
ADDER: Our clients are impatient. A / bargain: sink one, I name a crate.
TABITHA: I will sink all three and open / them myself.
FLAK: Adder? That was a good bargain. / Flak would take it.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M10 The Quiet Pass (Yellow Comet: Kanbei, Sonja; fog)

== m10_pre
@SONG aw2:422
NARRATION: The Comet Mountains. Fog lies in / the pass like milk.
LILI: I can see my own boots and / nothing else. Fog is a cheater.
TABITHA: Three roads cross the river: / west, centre, east. The summit is north.
TABITHA: A goat track in the far west, / foot only. Hold the summit and win.
LILI: A free port in the north buys its / grain through this pass.
TABITHA: Then we climb. Recons first. / The fog hides what is far.
NARRATION: Snow begins to fall on the pass, / thin and steady. Winter has come.
LILI(s): Cold. Fuel burns twice as fast. / Count your tanks!
KANBEI: Strangers in Kanbei's fog. This / border is his. Who are you?
TABITHA: Commander Tabitha of Novaria. / You will address me as "ma'am".
KANBEI: Travellers do not bring bombers, / Commander. Or siege guns.
TABITHA: We bring what we can carry. / Stand aside.
KANBEI: Eleven years Kanbei has kept this / summit. Not for coin. Not for courtesy.
TABITHA: Then I will walk through your / walls. And you.
SAMI: Don't scare them off, Kanbei! / Sami, Orange Star. West road's mine.
TABITHA: I seek no quarrel with Orange / Star. Yet.
SAMI: Then stay off my road. I'll be / polite once.
LILI: Put me on your march roll, please! / Mr. Bear signs for himself.
SAMI: You're on line one, little miss. / Under "creepy".
TABITHA: We climb at first light. Lili, / set the guns on the spur.
KANBEI: Let us see who breaks the silence. / It will not be Kanbei.

== m10_day4
NARRATION: Day four, the centre road. The guns / fall silent. A white cloth on the spur.
KANBEI: Commander. Your men carried his / wounded downhill.
TABITHA: They were in the way. Dead / soldiers block roads.
SAMI: Talk or fight, you two! Pick one! / I'm freezing!
KANBEI: One question, Commander. Why do / you march?
TABITHA: Because the bread stopped. And / someone will pay for that.
KANBEI: A conqueror who asks why. We / resume at noon.

== m10_post
@SONG co:kanbei
NARRATION: The summit. The fog thins, and / the Comet banner comes down.
KANBEI: The pass is yours, Commander. / A pass falls once. Kanbei learned.
TABITHA: Keep your honour. It weighs / nothing. Your men are alive.
KANBEI: Kanbei's own fields have borne / no wheat for two summers. Ash.
TABITHA: You have seen it too. So we are / all starving, then.
KANBEI: The Allies call it weather. / Kanbei calls it a debt with no creditor.
LILI: That is exactly what it is! May I / borrow your words? I will return them.
KANBEI: Then more than words. Kanbei asks / to follow your banner.
TABITHA: Two banners. Fail me and I break / you aloud, before everyone.
TABITHA: He rides in my Tag now. A useful / tool.
SOLDIER YC: Commander! A rider from the / ridge. A letter for Tabitha.
KANBEI: From Sonja. She never signs. She / only initials.
TABITHA: "The grey falls on my mountains / too. You are not alone.
TABITHA: Ask who clears the crates at the / northern Free Port. Not the yards.
TABITHA: Your name is in my black book. / Do not make me cross it out. S."

@SONG ally2

LILI: A ledger with a scary name. A / creditor who writes down every debt.
KANBEI: She does not forgive. She does not / forget. Kanbei trusts her.
TABITHA: You trust Sonja. How naive.
KANBEI: Kanbei trusts her to do what she / says. That is all of trust.
KANBEI: It is not warmth. It is / reliability.
TABITHA: Then the road runs to the Free / Port. Blockers get crushed.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M11 The Treasure Train (marauders: Lash, Flak)

== m11_pre
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The Pinewood road, in the north. / Forty tiles to the relay camp.
LILI: Four APCs! One chest! And eleven / ledgers from Kite Hill.
TABITHA: The chest feeds my garrisons. It / arrives, or someone pays. Slowly.
TABITHA: You ride the second truck with / the ledgers, Lili. Do not wander.
LILI: A truck has a roof and a little / ledger rail! Mr. Bear gets a window.
LILI: The Marrow cuts the road. Three / bridges: north, ours and south.
LILI: The ford is the knot. An APC stalled / on the bridge blocks all the others.
LILI: A camp road joins the bridges near / the camp. The convoy can choose.
NARRATION: The pines give out. Sand, scrub, / a ruin by the ford. A storm blows.
LILI: Dust takes a square off our guns' / range. And there is little cover.
TABITHA: Then the ford is the hinge. Hold / it. I will screen the road ahead.
TABITHA: Marauders. Lash holds the / north-east, Flak the south-east.
LILI: Somebody pays them! I heard the / price. It is a very good price.
FLAK: Hello, Novarians! Flak likes / convoys! Adder sold Flak's contract.
LASH: Flak! Don't greet the targets! / Heehee!
LASH: Four trucks, forty tiles, one ford. / I have read the map twice. Have you?
LASH: A convoy can't wait and a road / can't move. Q.E.D.!
TABITHA: We will see who read it better, / little man. Move out.

== m11_day5
NARRATION: Day five. The ford. The river / runs high and brown.
LILI: The ruin is a shortcut, Sister! / Nine hours saved. Hours are interest.
TABITHA: A ruin is a wall with holes. / Lash chose it for you.
LILI: ...Mr. Bear says the same. He is / never wrong. He is also stuffed.
LILI: Then I take the road. I took the / clever way once, at the Wall.
LASH: He chose the road! Boo! Fine, the / ford it is. Fire!

== m11_post
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The Directorate's relay camp. The / convoy rolls in at dusk.
LILI: Four APCs in, four out. I counted / them into the camp.
TABITHA: What do the ledgers say? Do / not bore me.
LILI: The grain contracts were all signed / on one day, on the Director's say-so.
LILI: The payee is the stencil, care of / a harbour office at the Free Port.
TABITHA: The port Sonja wrote about. Of / course.
LILI: The first contract is dated eleven / days after the grey harvest.
LILI: Nobody finds that much grain in / eleven days. It was waiting in a barn.
TABITHA: For us to get hungry. Father / signed every one. He lied to my face.
LILI: I do not say he read them. Daddy / reads everything, though.
LILI: He even read Mr. Bear's stitching. / It tickled.
TABITHA: Lili. Do not joke about him.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M12 The Iron Bastion (Blue Moon: Olaf, Andy; Tabitha + Lili)

== m12_pre
@SONG aw2:423
NARRATION: The Iron Bastion, Blue Moon. / Snow to the knee, rings on rings.
LILI: Two rings, Sister! The outer one is / iron: a pipeline wall, five gates.
LILI: The inner ring is the keep's rock, / four gates. The gaps are staggered.
LILI: I wish I were a rock. Rocks do / not worry.
TABITHA: You are not a rock. You break / rocks.
LILI: The wall has one seam, on the south / road. A missile silo guards the east.
TABITHA: In this Tag you swing the hammer / and I hold the line. Obey me.
OLAF: Wah-ha-ha! Visitors! Snow and / visitors! Olaf's two favourites!
OLAF: Olaf of Blue Moon! No gap lines up / with the next one. Wah-ha!
TABITHA: Commander Tabitha of Novaria. I / take this wall. Then the Accord's door.
ANDY: Hi! Andy, Orange Star! The Accord / lent me to the Bastion.
ANDY: I repair walls and guns overnight. / Hit one today, good as new tomorrow!
NARRATION: Snow is falling now, thick across / both rings.
LILI: The march slows and fuel burns / twice as fast. Brr!
LILI: I will bill you for the delay, / Commander Andy. Hee hee.
ANDY: Bill away, ma'am! Spare parts, / elbow grease, a smile!
OLAF: Take the hill if you can! Olaf / will wave from the top!
TABITHA: Lili. Plan the climb.

== m12_day5
NARRATION: Day five. The outer ring is / passed. The guns answer.
ANDY: There! The gun's back up! A little / oil and it sings!
LILI: He mended the gun I broke at noon! / A toy you can break twice a day. Yay!
LILI: Hit the repairmen first. Break the / walls by dusk, and none mend by dawn.
TABITHA: Tag Power on my word. You swing / the hammer.
ANDY: Aw, that's a clever lady! I'll fix / twice as fast! Watch me!

ANDY: I fixed that wall five times! Your / guns are so good, I'm a little jealous.
LILI: Commander Andy! The bill for the / delay. Mr. Bear signed it. Wobbly.

OLAF: Tell them Olaf held badly and / laughed! It's more truthful! Wah-ha!

OLAF: Go to the council! Nell is waiting. / Tell her Olaf lost to honest folk.
