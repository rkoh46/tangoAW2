# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M10 The Quiet Pass (Yellow Comet: Kanbei, Sonja; fog)

== m10_pre
@SONG aw2:422
NARRATION: The Comet Mountains. Fog / lies in the pass like milk.
AURELIA: I can see my own boots and / nothing else. I hate fog.
AURELIA: A wall I can measure. Fog is / a wall that moves.
CASSIUS: It is perfect for cavalry. / No one sees you coming.
CASSIUS: They also cannot see the / hole in the road. Details.
KLAUS: Three pass roads over a river: west, / centre, east. One summit, north.
KLAUS: A goat track in the far west, foot / only. Whoever holds the summit wins.
KLAUS: We have taken a coast, a fort and an / island. This is the roof of the frontier.
LIVIA: And the road to the next / ledger. The trail goes north.
LIVIA: A free port in the north buys / its grain through this pass.
KLAUS: Then we climb. Recons first. / Copters above the ridge line.
AURELIA: Fog grounds the copters at / the third cloud. I counted.
KLAUS: Then we climb on foot, and / learn the mountain's name.
KANBEI: Strangers on the Comet Road. / And in my fog.
KANBEI: I am Kanbei, commander of / the Yellow Comet border.
KANBEI: You are the green sun I read / of in Grimm's complaints.
KLAUS: Legate Klaus of Aurea. We / come as travellers.
KANBEI: Travellers do not bring / bombers, Legate. Or siege guns.
KLAUS: The roads here are steep. / We bring what we can carry.
KANBEI: A fair answer. It does not / open my pass.
KANBEI: Eleven years I have kept this / summit. I will not give it to a flag
KANBEI: that nobody has seen. Not / for courtesy. Not for coin.
CASSIUS: Your Grimm shouted at me / on the Comet Road. Politely.
KANBEI: Grimm shouts at everyone. / That is his gift to us.
KANBEI: I am quieter. You will find / my walls quieter still.
KLAUS: I do not seek your walls. I / seek the road through them.
KANBEI: Then we are agreed on the / difficulty. I will meet you at the top.
SONJA: Always so formal, Kanbei. / You will frighten the Romans.
SONJA: Greetings, travellers. I am / only watching from the north-east.
SONJA: A fog is a lovely thing. It / hides a great deal of dishonesty.
KLAUS: Whose, Madam?
SONJA: Everyone's. It is why I like / it. I shall be taking notes.
LIVIA: Notes, Madam, are a / liability. Mine are in cipher.
SONJA: Charming. Mine are in a black / book. You are in it already.
CASSIUS: I am famous! In a black / book! Put me on page one.
SONJA: You are on page three, / Prefect. Behind the legate.
KLAUS: We will climb at first light. / Aurelia, set the guns on the spur.
AURELIA: Setting them. If the fog / lifts I want to see him first.
KANBEI: Come, then. The pass has / been quiet for eleven years.
KANBEI: Let us see who breaks the / silence. It will not be me.

== m10_day4
NARRATION: Day four, the centre road. The guns / fall silent. A white cloth on the spur.
KANBEI: Legate. My scouts say your / men carried my wounded downhill.
KLAUS: They were in the way. I do / not leave men on a road.
SONJA: Do continue. This is the first / duel I have enjoyed all month.
KANBEI: One question, Legate, and I / ask it once. Why do you march?
KLAUS: Because the bread stopped. And / because someone made it stop.
KANBEI: A conqueror who asks why. / I still will not give you my pass.
KANBEI: Noon. We resume at noon, and / the best of us wins.

LASH: Flak. Do not greet the / targets. It ruins the calculation.

KLAUS: We will see which of us / has read the map better.
KLAUS: Move out. The chest does not / stop, and neither do we.
