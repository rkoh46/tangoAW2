NARRATION: The Accord's council hall, Orange / Star. Nell presides.
NELL: Order, please. The Allied Accord / hears Commander Tabitha of Novaria.
NELL: Commander, you have the floor. / Please don't take all night.
TABITHA: I will be brief, Chair. We came / for bread. We took forts on the way.
TABITHA: Three summers ago our harvest / failed. Ash fell on our fields.
TABITHA: Relief came from a freight house. / We followed its stencil.
TABITHA: It led to Black Hole's yards. Jars / of ash, not grain.
SASHA: Counterfeit coin. A counterfeit / story. Your quartermaster is charming.
LILI: Commander Sasha! I would love to / compare ledgers. Mr. Bear has his!
SASHA: ...That will not be necessary.
JESS: She kept the lanes clean at Isle / Verdant. Drake said so. I believe it.
MAX: I don't trust talkers. I saw her at / Ironhold. She fights fair.
NELL: The Accord has heard Commander / Tabitha. We don't vote yet.
NELL: Commander, you have safe passage in / our waters. Not our trust.
NELL: Bring me the one thing I can read. / A ledger, a name, a seal.
TABITHA: The trail runs to the Free Port. I / will bring you the book.
