# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M9 Grain Ships (Black Hole freight yards: Adder, Flak)

== m09_pre
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: Kite Hill, the northern coast. / Ten days until the tide.
LIVIA: Three barges at the quay. They / sail on the eleventh day.
LIVIA: The bill of lading says grain. / The creditor's stamp is the usual.
KLAUS: Then the barges are the road. / Whoever loads them, we meet.
AURELIA: The yards sit under a plateau. / A depot, an airfield, a ridge.
AURELIA: Take Kite Hill and you own / every approach to the water.
CASSIUS: Wings! You have an airport / and I have a bad feeling.
CASSIUS: Nobody taught my horses to / climb a plateau at a gallop.
KLAUS: Your horses hold the coast / road. Someone must.
CASSIUS(h): Holding. Splendid. I have / always wanted to be a gate.
LIVIA: Gates are paid by the day, / Prefect. I will see to yours.
AURELIA: Legate, look at the ridge. / Missiles, and flak tanks at the gate.
AURELIA: If the sky is theirs, we / are standing on stone.
KLAUS: Then we fight the ground / first and the sky second.
AURELIA: Bombs have a trajectory too. / Give me the angle and a map.
AURELIA: A bomber is only a very / expensive, very hurried shell.
KLAUS: You will have both. And / remember: these are not raiders.
KLAUS: Black Hole keeps its quay for / whoever pays. We are not paying.
LIVIA: I offered. They declined, / politely, and in advance.
KLAUS: Then it is a hill. Take the / depot. Sink the barges.

ADDER: Well, well. A green sun on a / white field. How very fresh.
ADDER: Quartermaster Adder, at your / service. And not at all at yours.
FLAK: Is it raiders, Adder? I like / raiders. They bring fuel.
ADDER: Flak, they are not raiders / until they have raided.

KLAUS: I am Legate Klaus of Aurea. We / do not wish a war with you.
ADDER: Delightful! A man with / manners. Then why the bombers?
KLAUS: Because your barges carry a / stencil I have followed an ocean.
ADDER: Ah. The stencil. A lovely / account. It pays well, and early.
ADDER: Early is the finest virtue. / We guard what early money buys.
FLAK: And I guard Adder. And the / sandwiches. Both are in danger.
LIVIA: Quartermaster. Name the buyer / and I will pay you double.
ADDER: Madam, I would sell my own / cape for double. I do not know him.
ADDER: Nobody does. The crates simply / arrive. The coin simply clears.
KLAUS: Then I will read the crates / myself.
ADDER: Read them from the lake, / then! Fighters up! Cape up!
FLAK: Up, Adder? I thought up was / for the planes.
ADDER: Metaphorically, Flak. Fire.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M10 The Quiet Pass (Yellow Comet: Kanbei, Sonja; fog)

== m10_pre
@SONG aw2:422
NARRATION: The Comet Mountains. Fog / lies in the pass like milk.
AURELIA: I can see my own boots and / nothing else. I hate fog.
AURELIA: A wall I can measure. Fog is / a wall that moves.
CASSIUS: It is perfect for cavalry. / No one sees you coming.
CASSIUS: They also cannot see the / hole in the road. Details.
KLAUS: Three roads and a goat track. / One summit. The Comet Pass.
KLAUS: Whoever holds the summit / holds the road to the next sea.
LIVIA: And the road to the next / ledger. The trail goes north.
LIVIA: A free port in the north buys / its grain through this pass.
KLAUS: Then we climb. Recons first. / Copters above the ridge line.
AURELIA: Fog grounds the copters at / the third cloud. I counted.
KLAUS: Then we climb on foot, and / learn the mountain's name.
KANBEI: Strangers on the Comet Road. / And in my fog.
KANBEI: I am Kanbei, commander of / the Yellow Comet border.
KANBEI: You are the green sun I read / of in Grimm's complaints.
KLAUS: Legate Klaus of Aurea. We / come as travellers.
KANBEI: Travellers do not bring / bombers, Legate. Or siege guns.
KLAUS: The roads here are steep. / We bring what we can carry.
KANBEI: A fair answer. It does not / open my pass.
KANBEI: Eleven years I have kept this / summit. I will not give it to a flag
KANBEI: that nobody has seen. Not / for courtesy. Not for coin.
CASSIUS: Your Grimm shouted at me / on the Comet Road. Politely.
KANBEI: Grimm shouts at everyone. / That is his gift to us.
KANBEI: I am quieter. You will find / my walls quieter still.
KLAUS: I do not seek your walls. I / seek the road through them.
KANBEI: Then we are agreed on the / difficulty. I will meet you at the top.
SONJA: Always so formal, Kanbei. / You will frighten the Romans.
SONJA: Greetings, travellers. I am / only watching from the ridge.
SONJA: A fog is a lovely thing. It / hides a great deal of dishonesty.
KLAUS: Whose, Madam?
SONJA: Everyone's. It is why I like / it. I shall be taking notes.
LIVIA: Notes, Madam, are a / liability. Mine are in cipher.
SONJA: Charming. Mine are in a black / book. You are in it already.
CASSIUS: I am famous! In a black / book! Put me on page one.
SONJA: You are on page three, / Prefect. Behind the legate.
KLAUS: We will climb at first light. / Aurelia, set the guns on the spur.
AURELIA: Setting them. If the fog / lifts I want to see him first.
KANBEI: Come, then. The pass has / been quiet for eleven years.
KANBEI: Let us see who breaks the / silence. It will not be me.

== m10_day4
NARRATION: Day four. The guns fall / silent. A white cloth on the spur.
KANBEI: Legate. My scouts say your / men carried my wounded downhill.
KLAUS: They were in the way. I do / not leave men on a road.
KANBEI: In the way. You say that as / though it were not honour.
KLAUS: Honour is a word for people / with leisure. I have a schedule.
SONJA: Do continue. This is the first / duel I have enjoyed all month.
KANBEI: One question, Legate, and I / ask it once. Why do you march?
KLAUS: Because the bread stopped. And / because someone made it stop.
KANBEI: Then you are no conqueror. / I still will not give you my pass.
KANBEI: Noon. We resume at noon, and / the best of us wins.

== m10_post
@SONG co:kanbei
NARRATION: The summit. The fog thins / and the Comet banner comes down.
KANBEI: The pass is yours, Legate. I / was taught that a pass falls once.
KANBEI: It is a hard lesson. I / thank you for the mountain.
KLAUS: You held it well. Eleven years / is a long time to hold anything.
KANBEI: I held it badly for the last / one. I knew it, and I held it.
KANBEI: My men followed me because / they trusted my face. I wronged them.
KLAUS: You wronged no one. Your / men are alive and so are mine.
KANBEI: That is the only honour / I have left. I will keep it.
AURELIA: Legate, he is crying. Is / that allowed in the Comet army?
KANBEI: In my army, it is a / courtesy to the dead. I am not ashamed.
CASSIUS: He is the best loser I have / ever met. I want lessons.
LIVIA: I do not think the lesson / is transferable, Prefect.
KANBEI: Klaus. I have heard you / on the radio, through the fog.
KANBEI: You said, "because someone / made it stop." I believe you.
KANBEI: My own northern farms are / grey. My fields in Comet Pass
KANBEI: have not borne wheat for / two summers. Ash. On the wind.
KLAUS: You have seen it too.
KANBEI: I have buried it. A farmer / carried a sack to me, weeping.
KANBEI: The Allies call it weather. / I called it a debt without a creditor.
LIVIA: That is precisely what it is. / I should like to hire your phrase.
KANBEI: Then let me offer more than / words. I ask to follow your banner.
KLAUS: You have a nation.
KANBEI: I have a nation that sent / me to hold a pass. I held it.
KANBEI: Now I choose where to stand. / Two banners, Legate. One road.
KLAUS: Two banners. I accept. / On the day my army is wrong,
KLAUS: you will say so aloud, in front / of everyone. That is the price.
KANBEI: That is the least I would / do. I accept the price gladly.
CASSIUS(h): I told you! He is better / than you. Give him a horse!
KLAUS: He has been given a / Tag. That is better than a horse.
SOLDIER YC: Commander! A rider from / the ridge. A letter for the Legate.
KANBEI: From Sonja. She never / signs. She only initials.
KLAUS: "The grey falls on my / mountains too. You are not alone.
KLAUS: Ask who clears the crates at the / northern Free Port. Not the yards.
KLAUS: Your name is in my black / book, Legate. Do not make me cross it
KLAUS: out. S."
LIVIA: A signed warning. How / unusually generous of her.
KANBEI: She is generous when she / chooses. She chooses rarely.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M11 The Treasure Train (marauders: Lash, Flak)

== m11_pre
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The harbour road, northern coast. / Twenty-six tiles to the relay camp.
LIVIA: Four APCs. One chest. Eleven / ledgers from Kite Hill.
LIVIA: The Senate's relay camp is at / the end of this road. We deliver.
CASSIUS: You are riding in a truck, / Quaestor. A truck!
LIVIA: A truck is dignified, Prefect. / It has a roof, and a ledger rail.
AURELIA: The road is a ribbon through / forest, one ford, one ruin.
AURELIA: The ford is the knot. Fifty / paces of water and no bridge.
AURELIA: An APC stalled in the river / blocks all the others. Remember that.
KLAUS: Then the ford is the / hinge. Hold it, if you can.
AURELIA: I can put guns on the east / bank. They will cover the crossing.
KLAUS: Cassius, you screen the road / ahead of the convoy.
CASSIUS: I am a screen. A glorious, / galloping screen. Tell me the enemy.
KLAUS: Marauders. A free company. / They sell their guns by the week.
CASSIUS: Which week is this?
LIVIA: A paid one. I have heard the / price. Someone is paying them well.
FLAK: Hello, Aureans! Everyone call / me Flak. It is nice to see a convoy.
FLAK: Adder sold my contract to a / company. They feed me. I say yes.
LASH: Flak. Do not greet the / targets. It ruins the calculation.
LASH: Four vehicles, twenty-six / tiles, one ford. A pleasing proof.
LASH: I have measured this road with / a ruler. You will lose it.
LASH: Lash. I read the map. The map / reads you.
LASH: A convoy cannot wait and a / road cannot move. Q.E.D.
KLAUS: We will see which of us / has read the map better.
KLAUS: Move out. The chest does not / stop, and neither do we.

== m11_day5
NARRATION: Day five. The ford. The / river runs high and brown.
CASSIUS: Klaus. The ruin is a short / cut. A hundred paces and no water.
CASSIUS: Lash is on the road. If we / break through the ruin, we beat him.
LIVIA: The shortcut saves nine hours. / Hours are interest, Prefect.
AURELIA: And a ruin is a wall with / holes. Lash chose it for you.
CASSIUS: ...Then I take the road. / Slowly. The way Klaus would have.
CASSIUS: I took the clever way at / Mistvale. I do not do it twice.
LASH: He chose the road. Disappointing. / The ford it is. Fire.

== m11_post
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The Senate's relay camp. / The convoy rolls in at dusk.
LIVIA: Four APCs in, four out. I / counted them into the camp.
LIVIA: A quaestor who loses count / of anything has nothing left.
CASSIUS: You rode in that truck the / whole way. Was it that dull?
LIVIA: It was restful. I read the / eleven ledgers. I recommend it.
KLAUS: What do they say?
LIVIA: That the Senate's grain / contracts were signed on a single day.
LIVIA: Each bears the Finance Board's / seal, on the Consul's authority.
LIVIA: The payee is the stencil, in care / of a harbour office at the Free Port.
AURELIA: The Free Port. The same port / Sonja wrote of.
LIVIA: The first contract is dated / eleven days after the grey harvest.
LIVIA: No freight house in the world / finds that much grain in eleven days.
LIVIA: It was already in the barn. / It was waiting for us to be hungry.
KLAUS: The Consul signed each one.
LIVIA: His seal is on each one. I / do not say he read them.
CASSIUS: Varro reads everything. He / reads my sealed letter when I sleep.
KLAUS: Cassius.
CASSIUS: I know. It is a joke. It is / a poor one. I am not laughing.
KLAUS: Lash and Flak have gone home. / The road is clear.
