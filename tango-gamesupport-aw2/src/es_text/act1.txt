# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Prologue

== prologue
@SONG opening
NARRATION: Aurea, beyond the Mare / Nostrum. Unseen for two centuries.
NARRATION: Then the first harvest came up / grey.
NARRATION: Ash on the wind, from the north. / Fields dusted like hearths.
NARRATION: The second harvest was greyer. / The third did not come.
NARRATION: Then the relief grain arrived. / Cheap. On time.
NARRATION: Crates from HOBB Grain and / Freight. No office, no owner.
NARRATION: A stencil on every crate, a / stamp on every bill.
NARRATION: Consul Varro found the money, / and did not ask where from.
NARRATION: The Senate was in debt to a / name. It sent two letters.
NARRATION: To Legate Klaus, in writing: / find who feeds us.
NARRATION: To Prefect Cassius, sealed: if / there is nobody,
NARRATION: take the north's granaries. Do / not wait for permission.
NARRATION: The legions sailed north under a / green sun on white.
NARRATION: They marched because the bread / stopped.

@SONG co:klaus
NARRATION: Cape Astra, Green Earth. Forty / days out of Aurelium.
LIVIA: The sea is flat, Legate. That is / the only good news.

AURELIA: The beach shelves at two fathoms. / Barges ground at forty paces.
KLAUS: Then forty paces it is. Is / Cassius not complaining yet?
CASSIUS: Not yet! My horses are still / being sick in the third hold.
LIVIA: He is on the radio. Pretend not / to hear him.
CASSIUS(h): I heard that. Klaus, one day / you'll say "well done, Cassius."
KLAUS: When I do, I will mean it. / Aurelia, show me the shore.
AURELIA: The fleet's sitting on a sandbar / a mile out. Past it, shoals.
AURELIA: Ports at both ends of the bar. / Then forty paces of surf.
AURELIA: Behind the sand, a coast road. It / climbs, with junctions, to the fort.
KLAUS: Cape Astra. Two coves, a headland, / and the only deep harbour for miles.
KLAUS: Whoever owns it owns the coast. / A landing is a claim. Make it.
LIVIA: It is also the last port of call / on every bill I own.
KLAUS: Explain, Quaestor.
LIVIA: The grain ships that fed us all / sailed past this cape.
LIVIA: Their pilots' logs are blank. The / harbour master's will not be.
KLAUS: Then we will ask him.
CASSIUS: Politely, I suppose. With a / basket of figs.
LIVIA: The figs are in the budget, / Prefect. They are the only line
LIVIA: that has not complained.

AURELIA: Two cruisers. Green Earth paint, / and they keep the lane well shut.
AURELIA: So the barges zigzag. Nobody / goes in a straight line today.
KLAUS: Hear me, all of you. This coast is / the first province.
KLAUS: We take the cape, then the road / behind it. Province by province.
KLAUS: These people have never seen our / flag. They see a fleet.
KLAUS: They will fight as any man fights / for his own front door.
KLAUS: I do not blame them. I will still / go through that door.
CASSIUS: Then let me ride ashore! One / charge and the fort is ours!
KLAUS: Your horses are sick. You land / with the second wave.
CASSIUS: The second wave! I was born for / the first wave, old man!
AURELIA: A horse in forty paces of surf is / an expensive way to drown.
CASSIUS: Don't make that face at me, / Aurelia. I can hear it.
AURELIA: I didn't say it. I was thinking / it very loudly.
KLAUS: Infantry and Mech first, with the / Recon beside them.
KLAUS: APCs carry the wounded. The / Anti-Air follows. Planes may come.

AURELIA: Two suns on one coast. Somebody / is going to get confused.
CASSIUS: Me. I'm going to get confused. / Which one do I hit?
KLAUS: The one shooting.
LIVIA: Legate. When this is over, the / Senate will ask what it cost.
KLAUS: Tell them it cost less than / hunger. Aurelia, signal the ramps.
AURELIA: Ramps down. May the sun be kind / to the first man ashore.
KLAUS: The sun is not what worries me. / That is a fort. Move.
