# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Prologue

== prologue
@SONG opening
NARRATION: Novaria, beyond the Silver / Sea. Unseen for two centuries.
NARRATION: Then the first harvest came up / grey.
NARRATION: Ash on the wind, from the north. / Fields dusted like hearths.
NARRATION: The second harvest was greyer. / The third did not come.
NARRATION: Then the relief grain arrived. / Cheap. On time.
NARRATION: Crates from HOBB Grain and / Freight. No office, no owner.
NARRATION: A stencil on every crate, a / stamp on every bill.
NARRATION: Dr. Caulder found the money, / and did not ask where from.
NARRATION: The Directorate was in debt to a / name. It sent two orders.
NARRATION: To his daughter Tabitha, in / writing: find who feeds us.
NARRATION: To Dr. Caulder himself, sealed: / if there is nobody,
NARRATION: take the north's granaries. Do / not wait for permission.
NARRATION: The Silver Nova sailed north / under a green star on white.
NARRATION: They marched because the bread / stopped.

== m04_map | TABITHA, LILI
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The Mistvale camp. Two corps / share a fire.
TABITHA: The frontier is forty leagues / north of the cape. Half the Second
TABITHA: helps us hold it. The other half / walks with Father.
LILI: The Second Corps wears red cloaks, / Sister. We wear green. Why?
LILI: Dyeing them costs forty-one credits. / Mr. Bear says they will miss the red.
TABITHA: Let them keep it. They will die in / it just as well.
LILI: Mr. Bear says that is the nicest / thing you said all week! Hee hee.
TABITHA: Mr. Bear is a fool.
LILI: He is stuffed, Sister. Not stupid. / That is a different thing. Pfft.
TABITHA: Is it? Father left his / instruments behind, I hear.
LILI: Eleven crates! Barometers and / something that ticks. All labelled!
TABITHA: Do not open the one that ticks.
LILI: Mr. Bear opened it! It was empty. / Was that the joke? I did not laugh.
TABITHA: Father does not joke. Not with / empty crates.
TABITHA: His order stands: if nobody is / found, he takes the north's granaries.
TABITHA: The north is full of granaries. / We have until the first frost.
LILI: Then the road is a clock! Mr. Bear / likes clocks. He winds them. Tick tock!
TABITHA: Then wind faster.
