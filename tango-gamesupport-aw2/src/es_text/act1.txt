# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Prologue

== prologue
@SONG opening
NARRATION: Aurea, behind the Mare / Nostrum. Two hundred years unseen.
NARRATION: Then the first harvest / came up grey.
NARRATION: Ash on the wind, from the / north. Fields dusted like hearths.
NARRATION: The second harvest was / greyer. The third did not come.
NARRATION: Then the relief grain / arrived. Cheap. On time.
NARRATION: Crates from HOBB Grain / and Freight. No office, no owner.
NARRATION: A stencil on every crate, / a stamp on every bill.
NARRATION: The Consul Varro found the / money, and did not ask whence.
NARRATION: The Senate was in debt to / a name, and it sent two letters.
NARRATION: To the Legate Klaus, in / writing: find who feeds us.
NARRATION: To the Prefect Cassius, / sealed: if there is nobody,
NARRATION: take the north's granaries. / Do not wait for permission.
NARRATION: The legions sailed under a / green sun on white, to conquer a road.
NARRATION: They marched because / the bread stopped.

CASSIUS(h): I heard that. Klaus, one day / you will say "well done, Cassius."

AURELIA: The fleet sits on a sandbar / a mile out. Past it, shoals.
AURELIA: Ports at both ends of the / bar. Then forty paces of surf.
AURELIA: Behind the sand, a coast road. / It climbs, with junctions, to the fort.
KLAUS: Cape Astra. Two coves, a headland / and the only deep harbour for miles.
KLAUS: Whoever owns it owns the coast. / A landing is a claim. Make it.
LIVIA: It is also the last port of / call on every bill I own.
KLAUS: Explain, Quaestor.

AURELIA: Ramps down. May the sun / be kind to the first man ashore.
KLAUS: The sun is not who I worry / about. That is a fort. Move.
