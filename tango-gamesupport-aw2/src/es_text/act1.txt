# Silver Nova dialogue, act1: Prologue, M1 Landfall, M2 The Ridge, M3 The Wall, M4 The Second Corps (format: README.txt)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Prologue

== prologue
@SONG opening
NARRATION: Novaria. Unseen by the world / for two centuries.
NARRATION: It lies far to the south, / across the Silver Sea.
NARRATION: Then its harvest came up grey. / Ash on the wind, from the north.
NARRATION: The second harvest was greyer. / The third did not come.
NARRATION: Then relief grain came. Cheap. / On time. Nobody asked from where.
NARRATION: Every crate bore the same stamp: / HOBB Grain and Freight.
NARRATION: Novaria's Directorate fell into / debt to that stamp.
NARRATION: Its head, Director Caulder, also / leads its army, the Silver Nova.
NARRATION: A scientist. His experiments / have no limits, and no ethics.
NARRATION: His daughter Tabitha leads his / troops. She despises the weak.
NARRATION: His youngest, Lili, was once his / experiment. Now she plays war.
NARRATION: The council sent one order: / find who feeds us.
NARRATION: Caulder added his own, sealed: / take the north's granaries.
NARRATION: The Silver Nova sailed north / under a silver sun.
NARRATION: They marched because the bread / stopped.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M1 Landfall (Tabitha; enemy Jess)

== m01_pre
@SONG co:tabitha
NARRATION: Cape Astra, Green Earth. Forty / days out of Port Argent.
LILI(h): The sea is flat, Sister! A good / day for breaking things. Hee hee.
TABITHA: A day for taking a harbour. Cape / Astra. My first province.
CAULDER: I am listening from my ship. Do / win nicely. I hate writing "failure".
CAULDER: The Second Corps lands a week / behind you, Tabitha. As agreed.
TABITHA: Stay behind, Father. I do not / share my beach. Or my glory.
LILI: Grain ships docked here. Pilots keep / no books. The harbour master does.
TABITHA: Then we ask him. Loudly. With guns.
CAULDER: Do keep him breathing. The dead / make poor data.
TABITHA: Report, Lili. The shore.
LILI: A sandbar sits off the cape, with / a port at each end. Then one road.
LILI: It climbs to the fort. Take the / fort, and the cape is ours.
LILI: Two green cruisers wait in the lane. / Fat little toys. They float. For now.
TABITHA: So we land under fire. Infantry / and Mech first, Recon beside them.
LILI: And the barges must wiggle! A / straight line is an easy target.
LEGIONARY: Commander! A banner on the fort. / Green, with a star on it.
LILI: Two stars on one coast. Which / one is the real one?
CAULDER: Neither, I suspect. Splendid. / Someone do write it down.
TABITHA: You will write "well done." / Signal the ramps, you dolt.
LILI(h): Ramps down! Tee hee! Let's go, / Mr. Bear!

== m01_day2
JESS: You, on my beach! Green Earth / Coastal Watch. Identify yourselves!
JESS: Your flag has a star on it. So / does ours. Ours is greener.
TABITHA: We are Novaria. We came for / answers. We will take your coast.
JESS: Answers don't come off a barge / with tanks on it.
JESS: You want to talk, send an envoy. / Unarmed.
TABITHA: I sent a white flag. Your cruiser / used it for target practice. Rude.
JESS: ...That one's on Drake. You still / aren't getting past me.

== m01_post
@SONG ally2
JESS(s): The fort's yours. My crews are / out the back. I'm the last one here.
TABITHA: Lay down your arms. A prisoner / is at least a little useful.
JESS: No. I'm leaving. We'll reload and / see you in the dunes.
TABITHA: Run, little warden. I will come / for you when I feel like it.
JESS: Huh. Invaders usually chase. / The last ones did.
TABITHA: I am not the last invader. / I am the best one.
LILI: Sister! The harbour master's book! / Every grain barge paid its fee early.
LILI: In full. No captain's name, no / flag. They are shy ships.
TABITHA: Somebody paid to be unseen. / Keep the book. Do not chew it.
CAULDER: The fort has fallen, I hear. I / missed it. How interesting.
TABITHA: Land behind me, Father. Cape / Astra is mine, and the coast with it.

== m01_map
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: Cape Astra in the evening. The / pier is Novaria's.
TABITHA: Lili, take a letter to the / Directorate. Cape Astra is taken.
TABITHA: No granary seen. No captain / found. The trail runs north.
LILI: "Taken" is a short word. The fort / was shelled twice. It cried a bit.
TABITHA: Write it anyway. And do not / draw on it.
LILI: Sister? The line that fed us has / no harbour. No captain, no office.
LILI: Who sends grain with no one on / it?
TABITHA: Someone who does not want to be / found. Somebody feeds me on purpose.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M2 The Ridge (Tabitha; enemy Eagle and Jess)

== m02_pre
@SONG co:tabitha
NARRATION: The Dune Road ridge. Eight days / to hold.
TABITHA: Lili. How long until the / causeway is finished?
LILI: The marsh ate the road, so the / piles need eight days. Eight!
LILI: If nobody sneezes. Ten if somebody / sneezes twice. Achoo!
TABITHA: Then nobody sneezes. We hold / this ridge until you finish.
TABITHA: The wardens' relief comes from / the north and east. Tanks and wings.
LILI: Our HQ is in the south-west, where / the dunes meet the marsh.
LILI: If it falls, we lose. Oh no.
LEGIONARY: Commander, the tank captain asks / to charge. That is the fourth time.
TABITHA: A charge uphill is a funeral. / He holds the reserve. Fool.
TABITHA: Tortoise formation. Guns on the / crest, shields roofed over.
TABITHA: A dune that does not run. Shells / roll off it like rain.
TABITHA: Each soldier holds her square of / sand, or answers to me.
LILI: Eight days, Sister. Mr. Bear is / counting.

== m02_day4
JESS: Tanks coming up the road! Same / place, Commander. Same tortoise!
EAGLE: Death from above! Oh, a / tortoise! Never seen one with guns.
LILI: Sister, planes! Four, maybe five. / Low and fast. Zoom, zoom!
TABITHA: Anti-air. Now.
LEGIONARY: Anti-air, form up! Missiles to / the crest! Roof high!
EAGLE: Huh. That's a really good tortoise. / Bombs away anyway!

== m02_post
@SONG ally2
EAGLE: Retreat! Not defeat, just / surprise... backwards!
JESS: Pull back! The dunes are yours, / Commander. Don't get comfortable.
TABITHA: The causeway is open. Eight days, / to the hour. Adequate. Lili.
TABITHA: By the authority the Directorate / gave me, you are Chief Engineer.
TABITHA: And Quartermaster. Do not get / cocky. Write it in the ledger yourself.
LILI(h): Me? In the mud, with no parade? / Does it come with a cake?
CAULDER: My congratulations, Chief Engineer. / The Directorate will claim it chose you.
LILI: Thank you, Daddy. ...Mr. Bear says / do not be nice. It is spooky.
CAULDER: Then I shall be nice quietly, / Lili. Good night. Do dream.
LILI: A dispatch case! "Unknown silver / fleet. Assume Black Hole." Who?
TABITHA: Black Hole? I do not know the / name. They fear it. And blame me for it.
TABITHA: They will not listen until I break / them. I enjoy that. The coast is mine.

== m02_map
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The causeway camp. A prisoner / is brought in.
SOLDIER GE: Please. Please don't eat me, / ma'am.
TABITHA: A starving prisoner is no use to / me. Sit. Eat. Do not drool.
SOLDIER GE: ...Really? They said you eat / your captives. Raiders. Silver fleet.
TABITHA: We take forts, not farms. My own / fields have gone grey.
SOLDIER GE: Ours too, ma'am. Two summers. / Ash on the wind, from the north.
TABITHA: Two summers. Ours was three. It / reached you after us.
LILI: The ash is a letter, Sister. Nobody / found the sender. Mr. Bear looked.
TABITHA: Corporal. Take a letter to Jess. / It says I wish to talk. For now.
SOLDIER GE: She'll shoot it out of my hand, / ma'am.
TABITHA: Then wave it and get shot.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M3 The Wall (Lili; enemy Javier, Drake's coast guns)

== m03_pre
@SONG co:lili
NARRATION: Muro Alto: walls, towers, a keep. / Rain since dawn. The wall is a smudge.
TABITHA: Muro Alto is the gate to the next / province. Lili, the wall is yours.
TABITHA: I hold the coast road behind / you. Try not to embarrass me.
LILI: Me? I lead? But Mr. Bear gives / the orders!
TABITHA: Give your orders to stone. Stone / is stupid. You will manage.
LILI(h): Stone does not talk back! That is / a good start. What toys do I get?
LEGIONARY: Artillery, Rockets, a Recon, / Infantry, and an airfield, Commander.
LILI: The wall has four gates. The keep / has one, in the south. Just one!
LILI: Com Towers make Javier's men / sharper. Capture them first.
LILI: A missile silo sits outside the / wall, on the west road. Nobody's!
LILI: Whoever stands on it fires it. Three / points to every unit near it. Boom!
LILI: I want it for the west gate, where / the wall guard is thickest.
TABITHA: Drake's Battleship sits offshore in / the north. It will shell you.
LILI: Its range is eleven. Mine is nine / and a half. Not fair!
LILI: And rain is fog. They see me, but / I cannot see one single gun. Boo.
TABITHA: Then the Recon goes first, and / slowly. Find the guns. Bombers after.
TABITHA: This is not a gift. It is a / test. Climb, or I leave you here.
LILI: Walls are easier to break than / to build. I like breaking. Hee hee.

== m03_day3
JAVIER: The gate is broken. Yet I swear by / the oath of the towers:
JAVIER: no tower falls while a knight / stands in its door.
LILI: The gate is gone, Lord Javier! It / is a broken toy. What does it say?
JAVIER: It says to stand in the doorway. / Which I am doing.
LILI: Behind that door is the whole / province. I need the door, please.
LILI: Mr. Bear says doorways do not / hold things up. I will knock politely.
JAVIER: Courtesy is the soul of a siege. / Come, Commander. Come. ...Child?

== m03_post
@SONG aw2:421
JAVIER: The keep is yours. I yield, not to / numbers, but to craft.
LILI(h): Thank you! It was a nice wall. Mr. / Bear and I are sorry we broke it.
TABITHA: Lord Javier. A question. You may / keep your sword. Toy.
TABITHA: The ash. Grey on the wind, grey / on the fields. Have you seen it?
JAVIER: For three years, in my family's / orchards. The trees blossom grey.
JAVIER: The scholars say the wind brings / it. They cannot say from where.
TABITHA: I do not care about your trees. I / care where the wind comes from.
JAVIER: Then we are not enemies in this, / Commander. Only neighbours.
TABITHA: We are enemies in everything. You / are only useful. For now.
JAVIER: I shall tell the Accord a corps took / a wall and spared its garrison.
LILI: Lord Javier? May I count the / orchards? I count nicely. Gently!
JAVIER: Count them, child. You will find / them all standing. Keep them so.

== m03_map
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: Muro Alto. A tent full of / crates and paper.
LILI: Sister, look! In the fort's store!
TABITHA: Grain crates. Stencilled. Even / their boxes are ugly.
LILI: The same stencil as the crates at / home! I counted the nails.
LILI: Forty yards, forty smiths, and one / box. Nobody builds like that.
LILI: Every bill in my ledger has the / same stamp. Paid early. Every one.
LILI: The debts are sold on before the / ink is dry. To who? Nobody says.
LILI: Eleven hundred bills. Not one / name. Where do they all go?
TABITHA: You stared at that stencil. Where / have you seen it? Speak, or I pinch.
LILI: ...Not on grain. I want to be sure / before I say. Mr. Bear says shh.
TABITHA: ...Then be sure. Quickly. I / am not patient.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M4 The Second Corps (Tabitha or Lili; enemy Caulder)

== m04_pre | TABITHA, LILI
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Mistvale, along the Verna. / A river under fog. Day one.
LEGIONARY: Commander! A corps on the east / bank. Orange colours. Masks.
TABITHA: The badge of the Second. It sailed / a week behind me. Behind!
LILI: It was to meet us at the cape! / Daddy said he was behind. Radio!
TABITHA: He was. Then he took the old road / and stopped being behind.
LILI: Why are they orange? We are silver. / Is the Second sick?
TABITHA: It wears Orange Star's colour here. / Tacky. And in fog, deadly.
LILI: Our toys will pick the wrong box.
TABITHA: Then fire only on what shoots. / Keep your Recons close.
LEGIONARY: A rider with a flag of truce. A / letter for "my dear Tabitha".
TABITHA: Give it here, you clumsy ox. / "To Tabitha, from Dr. Caulder:
TABITHA: The Mistvale road is mine, by seal / of the Directorate. Go round.
TABITHA: Postscript: my regards to Lili / and Mr. Bear. Your loving father."
LILI: He signs it "loving". That sounds / mean. The seal is real. I licked it.
TABITHA: A sealed order for him. For me, / nothing. He has stopped asking leave.
CAULDER: Tabitha. Did you like the / postscript?
TABITHA: Father, you block my road. What / does your seal say?
CAULDER: It says the Directorate is hungry, / and that you are slow.
TABITHA: That is not an order. What does / it order you?
CAULDER: The fog is lovely today. It makes / the data uncertain, and so honest.
CAULDER: Do be careful where you aim, girls.
TABITHA: Three bridges, no ford. Rout the / Second, or take its camp.
TABITHA: They are my soldiers. Spare / what you can. Lili. Do not whine.
LILI(s): I counted the cost of fighting our / own toys. I ran out of fingers.

== m04_day5 | TABITHA, LILI
LEGIONARY: Commander! A rider from the / Directorate! He is waving a seal!
TABITHA: Halt the line. All of it. Now.
CAULDER: Halt. His orders are for both of / us. Read yours, Tabitha.
TABITHA: "Command in the field stays with / each, as written."
CAULDER: "The Directorate will not choose / between its servants."
TABITHA: So the Directorate wants one of / us to lose. And it waits.
CAULDER: Fascinating... It prefers an / experiment to a decision.
CAULDER: We fight on, girls. I shall / note the results.

== m04_post | TABITHA, LILI
@SONG co:caulder
NARRATION: The fog lifts. On the east bank, / a white coat. A raised red glove.
CAULDER: You have beaten me, Tabitha. I / cut the corps in two. Half is yours.
CAULDER: A test, if you like. Soldiers are / only a variable. I have the data.
LILI: Three thousand one hundred and four / toys! Mr. Bear counted twice.
TABITHA: Father. The sealed order. Say it.
CAULDER: "If by the first frost no one is / found who feeds Novaria,
CAULDER: take the granaries of the north / by force. Tell no one."
LILI: I showed you, Sister. In the fog. / The copy came in the pouch.
TABITHA: I know. I wished to hear him / say it.
CAULDER: You find the hand. I find the / harvest. The first to succeed is right.
TABITHA: I trust you to want the harvest. / That is the difficulty.
CAULDER: How touching. Do stay useful, girls.

== m04_map | TABITHA, LILI
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: The Mistvale camp. Two corps / share a fire.
TABITHA: The frontier is forty leagues north / of the cape. Half the Second holds it.
TABITHA: The other half walks with Father. / Traitors.
LILI: They still wear orange, Sister. Dyeing / them silver costs forty-one credits.
TABITHA: Let them keep it. They will die in / it just as well.
TABITHA: Father left his instruments behind. / Eleven crates.
LILI(h): Barometers, and something that / ticks! Mr. Bear opened it. Empty.
TABITHA: Father does not joke with empty / crates. He measures.
TABITHA: His order stands. By the first frost / he takes the north's granaries.
LILI: Then the road is a clock! Tick, tock.
TABITHA: Then walk faster.
