# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Prologue

== prologue
@SONG opening
NARRATION: Novaria, beyond the Silver / Sea. Unseen for two centuries.
NARRATION: Then the first harvest came up / grey.
NARRATION: Ash on the wind, from the north. / Fields dusted like hearths.
NARRATION: The second harvest was greyer. / The third did not come.
NARRATION: Then the relief grain arrived. / Cheap. On time.
NARRATION: Crates from HOBB Grain and / Freight. No office, no owner.
NARRATION: A stencil on every crate, a / stamp on every bill.
NARRATION: Dr. Caulder found the money, / and did not ask where from.
NARRATION: The Directorate was in debt to a / name. It sent two orders.
NARRATION: To his daughter Tabitha, in / writing: find who feeds us.
NARRATION: To Dr. Caulder himself, sealed: / if there is nobody,
NARRATION: take the north's granaries. Do / not wait for permission.
NARRATION: The Silver Nova sailed north / under a green star on white.
NARRATION: They marched because the bread / stopped.

@SONG co:tabitha
NARRATION: Cape Astra, Green Earth. Forty / days out of Port Argent.
LILI: The sea is flat, Sister. Mr. Bear / says it is a nice day to break things.

LILI: The beach shelves at two fathoms. / Barges ground at forty paces. Tee hee.
TABITHA: Then forty paces it is. Is Father / complaining yet?
CAULDER: Not yet, my darling. My / instruments are seasick in the hold.
CAULDER: I have never seen a barometer / apologise. It was quite moving.
LILI: He is on the radio from the other / ship. The green one is not listening.
CAULDER: I heard that, my little one. Do / give the green one my regards.
CAULDER: One day, Tabitha, you will say / "well done, Father".
TABITHA: One day you will earn it. Lili, / show me the shore.
LILI: The fleet sits on a sandbar a mile / out. Past it, shoals.
LILI: Ports at both ends of the bar. / Then forty paces of surf.
LILI: Behind the sand, a coast road. It / climbs, with junctions, to the fort.
TABITHA: Cape Astra. Two coves, a headland, / and the only deep harbour for miles.
TABITHA: Whoever owns it owns the coast. / A landing is a claim. Make it.
LILI: It is also the last port of call on / every bill I own. Mr. Bear checked.
TABITHA: Explain.
LILI: The grain ships that fed us all / sailed past this cape.
LILI: Their pilots' logs are blank. The / harbour master's will not be.
TABITHA: Then we will ask him. Hard.

LILI: I packed a basket of figs. It is the / only line in the budget

LILI: Ramps down. Tee hee! Let's go, / Mr. Bear!
TABITHA: The beach is not a playground. / That is a fort. Move.

LILI: Mr. Bear opened it. It was empty. I / think that was the joke.
TABITHA: Father does not joke. Not with / empty crates.
TABITHA: His order stands: if nobody is / found, he takes the north's granaries.
TABITHA: The north is full of granaries. / We have until the first frost.
LILI: Then the road is a clock, Sister. / Mr. Bear likes clocks. He winds them.
TABITHA: Then wind faster.
