# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M5 Dust and Wheels (Lili leads; enemy Grimm and Sensei)

== m05_pre
@SONG co:lili
NARRATION: The Comet Road, Yellow Comet. / Day one of nine.
NARRATION: Four bridges over the Sable. / Posts: Highwatch, Crossways, Lowmere.
TABITHA: Lili. The corps has rested. The / road is yours. Do not waste it.
LILI: Mine, Sister? Then the clock first. / The Comet guards blow the bridges
LILI: after day nine. Two hundred sixteen / hours. Then the Sable is a river.
NARRATION: Dry sand country, scrub and rock. / A sandstorm walks along the road.
LILI: The dust hides us, and them too. / Long guns go blind. Wheels win!
LILI: The Comet Pipe walls the road. Gates / at Highwatch and Lowmere.
LILI: But the Crossways road has a weak / seam. One good hit and it pops!
TABITHA: A shortcut. Break the seam and the / wheels go straight through.
LILI: Each day we are late costs four / hundred measures of grain at home.
LILI: I do not like that number. It / makes my tummy hurt.
GRIMM: GRIMM! Who rides on my road? / Grimm blows every bridge first!
SENSEI: Don't shout, Grimm. They're not / on the bank yet. Look up, kid:
SENSEI: copters and paratroopers. Watch / the sky as well as the road.
LILI: Thank you, grandpa!
TABITHA: It was a warning, you dolt. Go.
LILI: Sister? If I fail the clock...
TABITHA: Then I tell the Directorate. / And Father.
LILI: Oh. That is what Mr. Bear was / scared of. ...Okay. Forward!

== m05_day4
SENSEI: Day four, and you still ride as / though someone is chasing you.
LILI(s): ...Somebody always takes the / toys, Sensei. So I hold them tight.
SENSEI: Hm. An old man's eyes are good for / one thing: faces. Yours is very young.

== m05_post
@SONG ally2
NARRATION: Day eight. Lowmere, the last / post, is taken. The bridges stand.
GRIMM: Grimm lost! Grimm has never lost / a bridge! Grimm is angry!
SENSEI: Hush, Grimm. The girl crossed / three hundred leagues in eight days.
SENSEI: In forty years I have not seen a / ride like it, Chief Lili.
LILI: ...You mean it? You are not just / being nice?
SENSEI: I am too old to be kind, kid. I / say what I saw.
SENSEI: Your people march because the / bread stopped. My villages say the
SENSEI: same of winter. Hm. Strange / company.
LILI(h): Sister! One day in hand! That is / four hundred measures of grain!
TABITHA: ...It was well done. There. / Now stop staring at me.
LILI(h): The nicest thing you ever said / to me!
TABITHA: It is the only one. Do not beg / for another.

== m05_map
@SONG co:caulder
NARRATION: Fort Amberlea's road, past the / Sable. A dispatch from the Directorate.
TABITHA: "Dr. Caulder to Commander / Tabitha. The Directorate approves
TABITHA: the road north, and the / quartermaster's ride." There is more.
CAULDER: Tabitha. I wished to say the rest / myself. The Directorate is pleased.
CAULDER: With the road. With Lili, who drove / three hundred leagues for bridges.
TABITHA: Who pays for the road, Father?
CAULDER: Novaria pays. The treasury is / open. Ask nothing about the coin.
CAULDER: Ask only for more road. A hungry / people forgives a long march.
CAULDER: Every province you take is a / granary I can name to the Directorate.
LILI: He did not say where the money / comes from. Mr. Bear noticed.
TABITHA: He was careful. He is never kind / for free. Write the figure down.
TABITHA: We ask again, in person. He will / squirm.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M6 The Ledger (Lili leads; enemy Colin and Hachi)

== m06_pre
@SONG co:lili
NARRATION: The market town of Argenthaven, / Blue Moon. Sixteen days.
TABITHA: The merchants wait. Talk, Lili. I / will stand behind you and glare.
COLIN: Oh! You're the Novarian / treasurer? I-I thought you'd be taller.
HACHI: The guild's always on time, / friend. It's your coin that's late.
HACHI: We bit it. Novarian silver is lead / with good manners.
LILI: The Directorate's mint made it. / I can show you. Look!
HACHI: Receipts I can't spend, kid. Show / me silver that rings true.
LILI: Then I will take what rings true. / Sister, the map, please.
TABITHA: The whole town is the target. / Seventy properties, fifty neutral.
TABITHA: Capture, nothing else. Hold / twenty-two at the end of any day
TABITHA: by day sixteen. Colin holds the / Guildhall, Hachi the Counting House.
LILI: The bosses' counters arrive on day / seventeen. If my book shows fewer
LILI: than twenty-two houses, they take / the money back. All of it.
LILI: Salt Row is cut off by the Mill / Water. Seven spans, and sluice gates.
COLIN: B-but we out-produce you three to / one! Look at my factories!
HACHI: Colin. Don't show 'em the / factories.
LILI: Thank you, Colin! Output, hours, / temper. That was three things.
HACHI: Big talk, kid. These deeds ain't / for sale.
LILI: Each house I take is a deed moved / over. A tank is a debt with a gun on it.
TABITHA: Advance. Weak merchants break / easily.

== m06_day6
LEGIONARY: Chief! A crowd at the counting / house! They say our bills are paper!
COLIN: A bank run! Sis taught me that / one! Your bills are dust!
HACHI: My till's been counting your bills / all week, kid. Slowly.
TABITHA: Stupid weak people! Panicking / over scraps of paper!
TABITHA: Open the vault and let them / choke on it.
LILI: No! Open every window. Pay every / bill in full, in silver. Slowly.
LEGIONARY: Slowly, Chief? The chest will / be empty in a day!
LILI: A run is only a queue. Queues get / tired and go home. Line up, line up!

== m06_post
@SONG aw2:423
NARRATION: Day fifteen. Argenthaven is taken. / Twenty-two properties on the ledger.
COLIN: T-twenty-two. She counted them / like beans, Hachi.
HACHI: Like debts. That's worse. I know / debts.
COLIN: Come to Blue Moon, Lili! A seat in / the guild! Half the profits!
HACHI: There's a clause, kid. You sell us / your grain contracts.
LILI: You have wanted them for months. No / sale. A clearing house, here in town.
LILI: Novarian grain, bought and sold in / guild coin. You get a customer who pays.
HACHI: Excellent deal. I don't like how / excellent it is.
LILI: I thought of an apology clause. / Mr. Bear ate it. Sorry.
HACHI: She's terrifying. Sign it, friend. / Before she raises the rate.
COLIN: Done! The Merchant Princes! Lili / and Colin! Best pair at sea!
TABITHA: My sister bought a guild with a / clause and a giggle. Efficient.

== m06_map
@SONG hagevolt

TABITHA: Is the coin accepted? Or do I / have to hang someone?
LILI: Accepted! Eleven hundred bills by / noon. Then I look. Come, look at this one.
TABITHA: A bill of exchange. Blue Moon / paper. It looks clean. Boring.
LILI: The ink is clean. It is the edge / I do not like.
TABITHA: ...A stencil. A circle, a bar, a / double stroke. Like the crates.
LILI: And under it, H, O, B, B. On a / guild bill three years old.

LILI: Or the name does the paying, / Sister. I do not know which. Not yet.
TABITHA: You went very white when you saw / it. Do not lie to me.
LILI: The room is cold. Mr. Bear says it / is not. Mr. Bear is being rude.
LILI: I will tell you when I am sure. / I promise.
TABITHA: When you are sure. Or I will make / you tell me.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M7 The Silver Sea (any lead; enemy Drake and Sensei)

LILI: The Twin Sisters are two little / islands. Each has a neutral airport.
LILI: Sand spits join them to our island / and Isle Verdant. Feet can walk!
LILI: The enemy can walk to the south / atoll the same way. Mind the reefs.
TABITHA: Reefs are for ships. Keep the / Cruisers in the open lanes.
TABITHA: The north island has a silo. It / takes three points from all around it.
TABITHA: Friend and foe alike. Fire it only / where the enemy is thickest.
LILI: The Green Earth fleet is the biggest / in two hundred leagues. Its flagship,
LILI: a Battleship, sits in mid-Roads. / Mr. Bear is seasick just looking.
DRAKE: Dude. The radio's on. I can hear / your seasick bear.
DRAKE: So why is a silver fleet here? / Everyone thinks you're the big bad.
TABITHA: Everyone is correct. I want the / harbour. For now.
DRAKE: Nobody wants a harbour "for now", / dude. Cannons say more.
SENSEI: Hmph. I've watched a hundred admirals / want one. The Comet wing flies from
SENSEI: Isle Verdant's airport. Mind your / hats.
TABITHA: Then the guns answer. Stand by to / be sunk, you fossils.
LILI: Anti-air cruisers on the left wing, / please, Sister.
TABITHA: Forward. Keep the lanes clear. / Fishing boats make dull targets.
