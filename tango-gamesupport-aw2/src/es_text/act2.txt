# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M5 Dust and Wheels (Cassius leads; enemy Grimm and Sensei)

== m05_pre
@SONG entry
NARRATION: The Comet Road, Yellow Comet. / Day one of nine.
NARRATION: Three bridges over the river. / Three signal-posts beyond.
KLAUS: Prefect Cassius. The legion / has rested. The road is yours.
CASSIUS(h): Say that again. Slowly. I / want the whole camp to hear.
KLAUS: I will say it once. You have / the command. The cavalry leads.
CASSIUS(h): He said it! Did you hear? / Klaus gave me an army!
AURELIA: He gave you a road and a / clock. Read the clock first.
CASSIUS: Chief, I read nothing. I / ride. Reading is for old men.
KLAUS: Then I will read it for you. / The Comet border guard will blow
KLAUS: the three bridges on day ten. / Beyond the river stand the posts.
KLAUS: Beyond them lies Yellow Comet. We cross / as conquerors, so we cross fast.
KLAUS: Take all three. If the bridges / fall, the legion waits for thaw.
LIVIA: Which costs, for every day of / delay, four hundred measures
LIVIA: of grain we do not have. I / shall not repeat the figure.
CASSIUS: Then do not! You are ruining / a perfectly good charge.
AURELIA: Timber and stone, forty fathoms / each. One keg at the pier
AURELIA: and the whole span is river. / They will do it, Prefect. Easily.
CASSIUS: Then we cross before they / light the fuse. Simple.
GRIMM: GRIMM! Who rides on my road / with a green flag?!
CASSIUS: Cassius of Aurea! And I / ride on it very fast!
GRIMM: Grimm will blow every bridge / before you breathe on one!
SENSEI: Do not shout, Grimm. They / are not even on the bank yet.
SENSEI: Copters and paratroopers, boy. / Look up as well as ahead.
CASSIUS: Listen! A grandfather who / knows my name!
KLAUS: He does not know your name. / He knows your horses. Go.
CASSIUS(s): Klaus. One thing. If I / am slow, if I fail the clock...
KLAUS: Then I will say so, plainly, / in the Senate. As I would of me.
CASSIUS: Good. That is... good. Right! / Forward! Nobody falls behind me!

== m05_day4

CASSIUS: Thank you! I do practise.

CASSIUS(s): ...What did you say, / grandfather?
SENSEI: Nothing. An old man's eyes / are good for one thing: faces.
CASSIUS: Well, I am running toward / the third post. Keep up, if you can!

CASSIUS(h): The clock! Aurelia! Tell / me the clock, loudly, to everyone!
AURELIA: Day eight, Prefect. Two days / to spare. I counted twice.
CASSIUS: Two days! Livia, how many / measures of grain is that?
LIVIA: Eight hundred. I have written / it down. It is a good number.
GRIMM: Grimm lost! Grimm has never / lost a bridge! Grimm is angry!
SENSEI: Grimm, hush. The man rode / three hundred leagues in nine days.
SENSEI: Cassius of Aurea. In forty / years I have not seen that ride.
CASSIUS: ...You mean that? You are / not just being kind?
SENSEI: I am old and cruel. I do not / do kind. I say what I saw.
CASSIUS(h): He said it. Did you hear? / A stranger! A Comet man!
AURELIA: We heard. The whole valley / heard. Please stop repeating it.
SENSEI: Your people march because / the bread stopped, they say.
SENSEI: My villages say the same of / winter. Hm. Strange company.
KLAUS: The border is behind us. The / first Comet province lies open.
SENSEI: Open, yes. Whether it welcomes / you is another matter. Hm.
KLAUS: Cassius. The posts were taken / with two days in hand.
CASSIUS: Say it, old man. Go on.
KLAUS: ...It was well ridden.
CASSIUS(h): HA! That is the best / sentence you have ever said!
KLAUS: It is the only one. Do not / wear it out.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- M6 Tribute (Livia leads; enemy Colin and Sasha)

NARRATION: The market town of Halcyon, / Blue Moon. Sixteen days.
KLAUS: Quaestor. You asked for me. / I would rather be at the front.
LIVIA: I asked you to be silent, / Legate. It will be difficult.
COLIN: Wowee! So you are the Aurean / treasurer! I thought you'd be taller!
LIVIA: And I thought the Blue Moon / guild would be punctual.
SASHA: The guild is always on time. / It is your coin that is late.
SASHA: We have examined it. Aurean / silver is not silver. It is lead.
COLIN: Counterfeit! Bold of you to / pay in it, honestly.
LIVIA: The coin is struck by the / Senate's mint. I can show you.
SASHA: You can show us a receipt. / We do not accept receipts.
LIVIA: Then I shall show you what / I do accept. Legate, the map.
KLAUS: The whole town is the target. / Fourteen properties, owned at once,
KLAUS: at the end of any day before / the sixteenth. Capture, nothing else.
LIVIA: The Senate's auditors arrive on / day seventeen. If I cannot show
LIVIA: fourteen properties on my books, / the credit is withdrawn. All of it.
COLIN: Ha! And we out-produce you / three to one! Look at my factories!
SASHA: Colin. Do not show them / the factories.
LIVIA: Thank you, Colin. You have / just told me three things.
COLIN: ...Did I? Sasha, did I?
SASHA: Yes. You always do.
LIVIA: Your hours, your output and / your temper. I shall use all three.
LIVIA: A tank is a debt with a gun / on it. I have settled larger.
SASHA: Then settle this one. We hold every / deed in Halcyon. Come and take them.
LIVIA: Gladly. Each capture is a deed / transferred, at the usual rate.
KLAUS: Advance.
