# BH2 dialogue: the prologue "The Auditor" (format: README.txt)
# The narration pages on the world map, then three scenes: the castle, the Allies in the east, the Obelisk where Sonja loses her name.

== prologue
@SONG prologue
NARRATION: The storm took four / nations. It took them gently.
NARRATION: Flags came down, one by / one. Not in fire. In order.
NARRATION: The last train left the / rail yard. Heavy. Very heavy.
NARRATION: A voice on the open band: / ALL COLOURS EAST. BUILD ANEW.
NARRATION: The fence is down. The old / continent lies open to the east.
NARRATION: There, past the maps, the / four nations first grew up.
NARRATION: There the Allies gather, / under a letter: "A."
NARRATION: In the west, the Obelisk / hums. A crate hums with it.
NARRATION: Lord Sturm sits in its hall / and listens. He does not smile.
NARRATION: He will not march east / himself. He wants a spymaster.
NARRATION: One woman has read the Accord's / secret files. All of them.
NARRATION: Yellow Comet's daughter has / not slept in one bed twice.
NARRATION: She is about to lose / her name.
NARRATION: The Auditor. / Black Hole 2.

== prologue_castle
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Orange Castle. Dusk. / Black Hole's flag hangs in the hall.
NARRATION: Three weeks of silence from / the east. Then one broadcast.
JUGGER: REPLAY. ALLIED BAND. VOICE / MATCH: ANDY. TEXT FOLLOWS.
JUGGER: "ALL COLOURS EAST. THE FENCE / IS DOWN. BUILD ANEW."
VON BOLT: Kehh... "Build anew." Who / writes slogans for a retreat?
HAWKE: Someone with a better hand / than Andy. An educated one.
HAWKE: Sonja. You know the Accord's / clerks. Who writes like that?
SONJA: Nobody I filed. A new hand, / raised after the fence went up.
SONJA: That is the part worth / fearing. I have no page on them.
STURM: The Allies run east with / their records and their children.
STURM: I do not chase. I hold what / is mine. They will come back.
CRUMB CO: Sir, may I ask? Why not / chase? Their train is slow.
STURM: A man who builds a fence / keeps his best things behind it.
STURM: The Accord's archives are in / the east. So is the reason for it.
HAWKE: Where the Accord wrote down / what it feared. And of whom.
STURM: Where it wrote my name, I / think. Before I had one here.
VON BOLT: Kehh... a free look at the / competitor's books! Priceless!
SONJA: Not free. Four armies. A / whole continent of locks.
STURM: Then I send someone who / reads locks. Not a general.
HAWKE: Lord Sturm. With respect. / She robbed a vault to join us.
HAWKE: She keeps records. And she / has never named her price.
SONJA: I told you. Fifteen per cent / of what the Colonel finds.
HAWKE: That was Von Bolt's price. / I asked for yours.
SONJA: You are right to ask, Marshal. / Ask me again tonight.
STURM: Tonight. At the Obelisk. / Bring your book.
CRUMB CO: The crate comes too, sir. / It hums a lot. It wants to.
VON BOLT: And second pick of the / east! In writing, Sturm!
STURM: Noted.

== prologue_allies

ANDY: Okay, that's the last wagon. / Count them again, Hachi.
HACHI: Counted twice, kid. Eleven / cars. Three hundred children.
HACHI: And a cargo nobody wants to / lift. I did not ask. I invoiced.
MAX: Pathetic, big man! The whole / fence on one train! I love it!
SAMI: Roger. But Nell is still in / that tower. We leave no one.
JAKE: Yo, we got the map in our / heads, Sami. Just give us a front.
RACHEL: By the book. First we count / our people. Then we count theirs.
ANDY: I didn't write the broadcast, / you know. Not one word of it.
ANDY: Somebody on the east side / handed me the page. "Read this."
RACHEL: Who?
ANDY: Never saw a face. Neat / handwriting. Signed "A."
HACHI: Whoever "A." is, she plans / ahead. The depots are stocked.
HACHI: Somebody paid for a whole / army's welcome. I know that price.
NARRATION: Far north-east, on the / Comet Chain, a lamp burns late.
KANBEI: Sensei. A letter from the / west, in Sonja's own hand.
SENSEI: Read it, Kanbei. I will not / look. Old eyes. Honest ones.
KANBEI: "Father. Thank you for the / scarf. I am well. Do not write."
KANBEI(s): Do not write. ...Then Kanbei / will set the table instead.
SENSEI: For her?
KANBEI: For whoever comes to it. / Honour has a place for everyone.

== prologue_turn
@SONG own:door
NARRATION: The Obelisk. Midnight. The / crate hums. The air is thin.
STURM: Your book. Open it.
SONJA: Page one is a list of men / who would not leave a soldier.
SONJA: The rest is the Accord. Every / nation. Every clerk. Every seal.
SONJA: I joined you to read the one / file my father forbade me.
SONJA: The Accord's own. The pages / it buried when it built the fence.
HAWKE: At last. And what do you / want in return?
SONJA: The truth, Marshal. And the / name of whoever hid it.
CRUMB CO: Ma'am? The crate went quiet / when you said "truth".
SONJA: So it did, Commander.
STURM: The Allies hold a file on you / in four languages.
HAWKE: Forty files. Every ministry / knows her face. And her father's.
SONJA: They will say Yellow Comet / sent a spy. They will say it of him.
SONJA: Nobody can say Yellow Comet's / daughter did it, if she is gone.
NARRATION: She takes a small black / book from her coat. Strikes an S.
SONJA(s): Sonja watched over one small / country. It was a kind one.
SONJA: It belonged to a man who / loves me. I will not spend him.
STURM: A name is a possession. / Who owns yours.
SONJA: Nobody. As of this minute.
VON BOLT: A NEW name! Kehh! There is / a fee for a new name!
HAWKE: What do we call you.
SONJA: My mother's word for me, / before I was a general's daughter.
SONJA: Asuka. Only my father will / know it. Let him.
CRUMB CO(h): It's a good name, ma'am! / Gerald says it sounds like morning.
NARRATION: The hum drops half a tone. / Gerald's mark warms.
STURM: Asuka. You hold the eastern / war. You answer to me.
ASUKA: I will see it done, Lord Sturm. / Quietly, and in order.
HAWKE: I do not trust you.
ASUKA: Good, Marshal. Keep your / suspicion. You are right to have it.
HAWKE: A new name for an old / ambition. Very well, Asuka.
HAWKE: Do not disappoint us.
STURM: Take the crate. Take the boy. / Whatever answers it, bring to me.
ASUKA: Whatever answers, Lord Sturm, / I will hear it before you do.
STURM: Noted.
