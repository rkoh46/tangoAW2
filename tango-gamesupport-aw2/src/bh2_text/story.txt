# BH2 dialogue: the prologue "The Auditor" (format: README.txt)
# The narration pages on the world map, then three scenes: the castle, the Allies in the east, the Obelisk where Sonja loses her name.

== prologue
@SONG prologue
NARRATION: Four nations fell to the storm. / It never had to hurry.
NARRATION: Their flags came down one at / a time. Quietly. In order.
NARRATION: The last Allied train left / the station, heavy and slow.
NARRATION: Then a voice on the open band: / ALL COLOURS EAST. BUILD ANEW.
NARRATION: The fence was gone. East lay / the old continent.
NARRATION: Beyond the edge of the maps, / where the four nations began.
NARRATION: The Allies gathered there under / someone who signed only "A."
NARRATION: In the west the Obelisk / hummed, and a crate hummed back.
NARRATION: Lord Sturm sat in its hall / and listened. He did not smile.
NARRATION: He would not cross the sea / himself. He needed a spy.
NARRATION: One woman had read every / secret file the Accord kept.
NARRATION: Yellow Comet's daughter, who / never slept in one bed twice.
NARRATION: She was about to throw / her own name away.
NARRATION: The Auditor. / Black Hole 2.

== prologue_castle
@SONG enemy1
NARRATION: The Orange Castle at dusk. / Black Hole's flag hangs in the hall.
JUGGER: PLAYBACK. ALLIED BAND. VOICE: / ANDY. "BUILD ANEW."
VON BOLT: Kehh... "Build anew." Who / puts a slogan on a retreat?
HAWKE: Not Andy. That is a writer's / work. A careful one.
HAWKE: Sonja, you know their clerks. / Who writes like that?
SONJA: Nobody in my files. Someone / who came up after the fence.
STURM: I will not chase them. I keep / what is mine. They will return.
CRUMB CO: Sir? Why not chase them? / That train is slow.
STURM: A man who builds a fence keeps / his best things behind it.
STURM: The Accord's archive went east. / So did the reason it exists.
HAWKE: Everything the Accord ever / feared, on paper. And who.
STURM: My name is in there. From / before I had one in this world.
STURM: I will send someone who opens / doors. Not a general.
HAWKE: Lord Sturm, with respect, she / robbed a vault to join us.
HAWKE: She keeps notes on all of us. / And she never named her price.
SONJA: Fair question, Marshal. Ask me / again tonight.
STURM: Tonight, then. At the Obelisk. / Bring your book.
CRUMB CO: Should I bring the crate, sir? / It's been humming since lunch.
VON BOLT: And I get second pick of / the east! I want it in writing!
STURM: Noted.

HACHI: Twice already, kid. Eleven / cars. Three hundred children.
HACHI: And some cargo nobody wants to / touch. I didn't ask. I charged.

HACHI: Whoever "A." is, she plans / ahead. The depots are full.

KANBEI: Sensei. A letter from the / west. Sonja's own hand.

KANBEI(s): Do not write. ...Then Kanbei / will set a place for her at table.

== prologue_turn
@SONG own:door
NARRATION: The Obelisk at midnight. The / crate hums. The air feels thin.
STURM: Your book. Open it.
SONJA: The rest is the Accord. Every / nation, every clerk, every seal.
SONJA: I came to read the one file my / father kept from me.
HAWKE: Finally. And what do you / want for it?
SONJA: The truth, Marshal. And the / name of whoever hid it.
SONJA: They will blame my father. They / cannot blame a daughter who is gone.
NARRATION: She takes a small black book / from her coat. Crosses out an S.
SONJA(s): Sonja served one small / country. A kind one.
SONJA: Then I take what my mother called me, / before I was a general's girl.
SONJA: Asuka. Only my father will / know it. That is enough.
CRUMB CO(h): It's a nice name, ma'am! / Gerald says it sounds like morning.
NARRATION: The hum drops half a tone. / Gerald's mark grows warm.
STURM: Asuka. The war in the east / is yours. You answer to me.
ASUKA: It will be done, Lord Sturm. / Quietly, and in order.
HAWKE: I do not trust you.
ASUKA: Good. Do not start now, / Marshal. You would be wrong to.
STURM: Take the crate. Take the boy. / Whatever answers it, bring to me.
ASUKA: Whatever answers, Lord Sturm, / I will hear it before you do.
STURM: Noted.
