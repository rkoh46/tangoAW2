@SONG ending1
NARRATION: Dawn in the Last Citadel's / Great Hall. Four banners hang as one.

HAWKE: When the army says so, / Commander. Not before.
KANBEI: Kanbei has one more matter. A / table is set. The seventh chair is warm.

KANBEI: Kanbei will keep the soup warm. / However long it takes.
