# BH2 Campaign — design draft 1 (for review)

Lead designer's draft built on the owner's founding notes (2026-10-28). Nothing is built yet: story, cast and map plans are for review first.

## Founding notes (the owner's, fixed)

- You play as **Asuka**. Sonja appears only in the prologue, before she becomes Asuka.
- **Story:** it continues BH1's secret ending. A proper prologue covers the defection. Asuka leads Black Hole, gathers intel and invades every country. Every BH1 cliffhanger is wrapped up.
- **Missions:** 25, plus **secret M26**, unlocked after M24. In M26 Black Hole fights **Emerald Sun** for the first time.
- **World map:** Advance Wars 1's.
- **Final mission (M25):** the hardest yet. A very big map with no time limit.
- **Content:**
  - Black Hole factories in some missions, and exactly **3 two-front missions**.
  - Lasers, Black Crystals, Black Obelisks, Black Cannons and minicannons, against enemies strong enough to take them.
  - The **Deathray** always sits at the top of its map.
- **Presentation:** music in every scene, a proper prologue and separate ending credits.
- **Maps:** design them from real references (AWBW). No cut-off rivers and no bad terrain, enforced by the kit's lint and render checks.

## 1. The premise in one paragraph

BH1 ended with Andy's broadcast: **"ALL COLOURS EAST. THE FENCE IS DOWN. BUILD ANEW."** The Allies took the last train and their ships **east, past the maps**, to the old continent on the AW1 map, where the four nations first grew up. Sturm holds the west and sits in the Obelisk's hall listening to the crate hum. He gives the eastern war to the one officer who already knows the Allies' ledgers from the inside: **Asuka**, the woman who was Sonja. Her method is intel first, then invasion. Black Hole lands on the grey island in the south-west, **the Ashen Shelf**, the one place on the AW1 map no nation owns. From there it takes Green Earth's islands, the Yellow Comet chain, the Blue Moon north and the Orange Star capital where the Accord has been rebuilt. On the way, every BH1 mystery is opened, and the last one opens a door.

## 3. Cast and rivalries

**Black Hole: Asuka leads in every mission.** The other twelve all get story missions as her second front, her tag partner, or a solo detachment she commands by radio:
- Sturm, Von Bolt, Hawke, Koal, Kindle, Jugger, Flak, Lash, Adder, Clone Andy and Crumb.
- Sturm is not playable until the end. You face him in M25. He is playable in Free Play afterwards.

**The Allies, all 18 present:** Andy, Max, Sami, Nell, Hachi, Jake, Rachel, Olaf, Grit, Colin, Sasha, Kanbei, Sensei, Grimm, Eagle, Drake, Jess and Javier.

**Counts**
- Two-front missions: 3 (M4, M10, M20).
- Factory missions:
  - your Black Factory: M3, M14 and M22;
  - the enemy's: M25.
- Inventions: in about 12 missions.
- Deathray: M25 only, at the top of the map.
- Forced CO picks: about 12, the rest free.
- Day limits: only where the clock is the mission (M2, M7, M16, M20).

## 5. Ending and credits

- **Epilogue after M25.** The door is shut with Crumb's key. Sturm is **sealed beyond it, not killed**: an open door for the future. Asuka leads Black Hole with Hawke as Marshal, and "Succession" ends with Hawke kneeling to the army, not the person. Nell and Andy sign a truce with Black Hole, the first new Accord in thirty years. Crumb keeps Gerald and the key.
- **Separate credits sequence.** A full staff roll with its own music, cast by army, and a last page.
- **Secret epilogue after M26.** Emerald Sun's legion halts at the edge of the map. Klaus: "The sun does not set. It waits." This links to the Emerald Sun Campaign.

## 6. Music

Every scene has a song (the kit's lint enforces it). The music plan:
- **Asuka's scenes:** "Iron Verdict".
- **Each enemy CO's scenes:** that CO's own theme.
- **Prologue:** a quiet piece.
- **Crate and door scenes:** a new "Door" motif.
- **Final mission:** a new song, "Storm Unbound".
- **Credits:** a new song.

The new songs follow the AW2 Black Hole style the music kit now measures, with lettered sketches first.

## 7. World map

There is no AW1 map in your ROMs (AW2 and DS only), and the engine draws only the AW2 and Omega Land maps. I suggest building the AW1 map from your picture: a new world-map type that takes it as the base and fits it to the GBA screen in AW2's palette, with region flags and mission markers. That matters because I don't draw art. If you have a cleaner picture or a real AW1 world-map screenshot, I'd use that instead.
