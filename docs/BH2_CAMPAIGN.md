# BH2 Campaign — design draft 1 (for review)

Lead designer's draft built on the owner's founding notes (2026-10-28). Nothing is built yet: story, cast and map plans are for review first.

## Founding notes (the owner's, fixed)

- You play as **Asuka**. Sonja appears only in the prologue, before she becomes Asuka.
- **Story:** it continues BH1's secret ending. A proper prologue covers the defection. Asuka leads Black Hole, gathers intel and invades every country. Every BH1 cliffhanger is wrapped up.
- **Missions:** 25, plus **secret M32**, unlocked after M29. In M32 Black Hole fights **Emerald Sun** for the first time.
- **World map:** Advance Wars 1's.
- **Final mission (M30):** the hardest yet. A very big map with no time limit.
- **Content:**
  - Black Hole factories in some missions, and exactly **3 two-front missions**.
  - Lasers, Black Crystals, Black Obelisks, Black Cannons and minicannons, against enemies strong enough to take them.
  - The **Deathray** always sits at the top of its map.
- **Presentation:** music in every scene, a proper prologue and separate ending credits.
- **Maps:** design them from real references (AWBW). No cut-off rivers and no bad terrain, enforced by the kit's lint and render checks.

## 1. The premise in one paragraph

BH1 ended with Andy's broadcast: **"ALL COLOURS EAST. THE FENCE IS DOWN. BUILD ANEW."** The Allies took the last train and their ships **east, past the maps**, to the old continent on the AW1 map, where the four nations first grew up. Sturm holds the west and sits in the Obelisk's hall listening to the crate hum. He gives the eastern war to the one officer who already knows the Allies' ledgers from the inside: **Asuka**, the woman who was Sonja. Her method is intel first, then invasion. The campaign **starts on Black Hole's own island**, the grey island in the bottom-left corner of the map: Black Hole territory, the base of the invasion. From there it takes Green Earth's islands, the Yellow Comet chain, the Blue Moon north and the Orange Star capital where the Accord has been rebuilt. On the way, every BH1 mystery is opened, and the last one opens a door.

## 3. Cast and rivalries

**Black Hole:** Asuka is the **lead commander of the invasion** and the story's voice. As in BH1, you pick any Black Hole CO in most missions, and some missions force a CO where the story or a rivalry needs one (about 14 forced, 12 free). The other twelve all get story missions as her second front, her tag partner, or a solo detachment she commands by radio:
- Sturm, Von Bolt, Hawke, Koal, Kindle, Jugger, Flak, Lash, Adder, Clone Andy and Crumb.
- Sturm commands in the prologue, fights beside Asuka in **M5 (forced Sturm + Asuka)** and leaves through the door in M28. He is in Free Play afterwards.

**The Allies, all 18 present:** Andy, Max, Sami, Nell, Hachi, Jake, Rachel, Olaf, Grit, Colin, Sasha, Kanbei, Sensei, Grimm, Eagle, Drake, Jess and Javier.

## 4c. Map rules for every mission (owner, 2026-10-28)

These apply to all 32 missions, not just Act I:
1. **Ports** always touch navigable sea a ship can leave by (PT01 is an error).
2. **Black Factories** must spawn, and spawn smart, proven in the harness with a spawn log.
3. **Inventions** never hit anyone on day 1: no unit starts in a Laser's lines or a cannon's first target area. They are Black Hole's alone. The Deathray, where used, goes at the top of the map.
4. **Looks and weather vary,** and neighbouring missions never share both.
5. **Maps look designed, not generated:**
   - roads follow the terrain;
   - coasts have bays and capes;
   - cities sit in towns worth fighting over;
   - mountains and woods form ridges, passes and forest belts;
   - no filler.
6. **Every property and terrain type gets used** across the campaign: Com Towers, silos, ports, airports, pipes and seams, reefs, rivers and bridges, volcanoes.
7. **Rivers are never cut off,** beaches are long and Landers can land. Each map's picture is checked against the game's own tiles (RN01).

## 5. Ending and credits

- **Epilogue after M30.** The new Orange Star CO is the last to fall. Nell surrenders the Accord's seal to Asuka, not to Sturm. Asuka leads Black Hole and the world, with Hawke as Marshal; "Succession" ends with Hawke kneeling to the army, not the person. Crumb keeps Gerald and the key, and the door hums, closed but not locked. Sturm is somewhere past it.
- **Separate credits sequence.** A full staff roll with its own music, cast by army, and a last page.
- **Secret epilogue after M32.** Emerald Sun's legion halts at the edge of the map. Klaus: "The sun does not set. It waits." This links to the Emerald Sun Campaign.

- **Asuka's scenes:** "Iron Verdict".
- **Each enemy CO's scenes:** that CO's own theme.
- **Prologue:** a quiet piece.

- **Credits:** a new song.

The new songs follow the AW2 Black Hole style the music kit now measures, with lettered sketches first.

## 7. World map (decided)

The owner's AW1-style picture is imported pixel by pixel as a new interactive world map. That means a new world-map type in the engine: the picture in GBA colours, region flags per nation, mission markers and the cursor, as AW2's map works. No AW1 ROM is needed.

## 8. Decisions (owner, 2026-10-28)

1. **The final enemy is not Sturm.** M30 is a scripted gauntlet: Nell, then Olaf, Kanbei and Eagle take over in turn, with growing reinforcements. It works like BH1's Nell-to-Andy takeover.
2. **The lore answers in section 2 are approved.**
3. **No truce against Sturm.** He leaves through the door in M28.
4. **The owner's picture is the world map**, imported pixel by pixel.
5. **Three new songs (Door motif, final battle, credits):** sketches first, for the owner to listen to.
6. **The owner's new CO:** concepts are offered for the owner to choose from.
7. **No Deathray or other Black Hole invention in Allied hands (owner).** The final mission has no Deathray.
8. **M30's last enemy is a new Orange Star CO,** after Nell, Olaf, Kanbei and Eagle. Lead designer's concept below; the owner supplies the art.

## 9. The new Orange Star CO (concept for review)

**Aria, Commander of the New Accord.** Aria is **Nell's sister** (owner, 2026-10-28) and the first officer raised east of the fence. She rebuilt the Allied army while Nell was a prisoner. She wrote the words of Andy's broadcast, "BUILD ANEW", and believes in the Accord's ideal even after Asuka's ledger exposed its lie. She is Asuka's mirror: what Sonja might have become if she had stayed. She appears in Act IV scenes (planning Nell's rescue in M21, the war room after M25), so M30's reveal is earned.
- **Day to day, "Common Cause":** her units are +10% defence, and +10% more next to another of her units.
- **CO Power, "Rally":** all units heal 1 HP and move 1 more space.
- **Super CO Power, "Build Anew":** all units heal 3 HP and gain +20% attack and +30% defence. Every city and HQ she owns produces funds twice that day.
- **Theme:** an Orange Star hero theme of her own: bright, determined, the last stand.
- **Nell's sister (decided).** Her look comes from the owner's art.
