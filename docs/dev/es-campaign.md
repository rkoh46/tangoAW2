# The Emerald Sun Campaign: building and checking it (`aw2dev maps gen`, `aw2dev campaign sheets | sim | difficulty | check`)

Sixteen missions played as Emerald Sun, the second custom campaign on the BH Campaign's engine. The story and the approved design are `docs/ES_CAMPAIGN.md`; this file is how it is built
and how every part is checked. Skill: `aw2-es-campaign`. Status: playable behind the development flag `TANGOAW2_ES_CAMPAIGN` (the Campaign sub-menu still says "Coming soon"
in a normal build; the flag turns it into a source of `campaign_model::SOURCES`); nothing is pushed or released until the user approves.

## Rules the campaign keeps (the user's)
- One battlefield a mission: **no two-front engine**. Tag missions (pairs) are fine (M8, M12, M13, M15).
- **Black Hole's inventions only in the two missions against Black Hole** (M9 Grain Ships, M13 The Free Port): the map file's `blackhole` line tags them; rule **ES01** of `aw2dev maps lint --source es`
  fails an invention tile anywhere else and a third tagged map; `aw2dev campaign check` repeats it. M16's toughness comes from army size, production, funds, unit quality, fortified terrain, waves, Varro's powers.
- **M16 is the hardest mission by a clear margin** (`aw2dev campaign difficulty --require-final`); M14 and M15 ramp up to it.
- **An army stops at 50 units** (AW2: its production and a trigger's reinforcements stop there; the sim saw Varro, started at 50, build and receive nothing): no deployment above 40 (the Rust test `no_army_starts_with_more_than_40_units`, the python test, `campaign check`).
- The player's army wears the Emerald Sun skin on army 1's colour, always one of AW2's four (Orange Star's mostly, Blue Moon's or Green Earth's where an enemy wears red) and never one an enemy or ally wears: a skin on colour 5 in a
  game that is not five-army corrupted memory when the army bought a unit (found by the mission sim, test `no_army...` / `the_player_wears_a_colour_of_its_own`). Only one army wears it: Cassius's Second Legion (M4), Varro's Praetorians and
  the Accord's fleet wear the stand-in colours.
- Every unit deployed starts at full HP, ammo and fuel (`es_boot_m*` checks it); beaches are generous (RC04); every mission's story scenes have a song (`tools/estext/lint.py`, `campaign scenes --campaign es`).

## The kit
| Command | What |
|---|---|
| `aw2dev maps gen [--map es01 ..] [--no-build] [--check]` | runs the specs (`five/es/spec/*.py`: `Canvas` paints terrain, roads, rivers, blobs, scatter, properties, units, notes), writes `five/es/esNN.txt`, builds `src/es_map_data.rs` (tile joins checked as for the BH maps). `--check` fails when a text map differs from its spec |
| `aw2dev maps lint --source es [--map esNN] [--warn]` | the BH Campaign's rules on the ES maps (+ ES01); the picture is `devkit/work/maps/lint/es/esNN.png` |
| `python3 tools/estext/lint.py [act1..act4] [--counts]` | the dialogue linter: grammar, every box two lines of 176 px (AW2's font from your ROM), voice slips (Klaus and Livia never shout or contract, Varro never shouts, nobody says who HOBB is), songs known and present, every key asked for by the code |
| `aw2dev campaign scenes --campaign es` | every scene with its song and box count |
| `aw2dev campaign sheets [--mission N ..]` | **review sheets**: the game's own whole-map picture of every mission (fog OFF; a fog mission also fogged), "MISSION N - Title" burned in, a contact sheet per act (`devkit/work/campaign/es/sheets/`) |
| `aw2dev campaign sim --mission N .. [--player bot cpu] [--seeds K] [--days D] [-j J]` | the **mission sim**: the test player (`aw2test.bot`, pad inputs only) or the game's computer playing both sides, from a record with the earlier missions won; one row each (result, days, units left, funds); appended to `devkit/work/campaign/es/sim.jsonl` |
| `aw2dev campaign difficulty [--require-final]` | the **difficulty score**: static (enemy start + reinforcement value against the player's, funds and ten days' income, the share that holds, lanes of attack) blended with the sims' results (CPU-vs-CPU win rate, days the test player needs); M16 must lead by 10% |
| `aw2dev campaign check` | the static rules: 16 missions, inventions only in M9 and M13, unit caps, a Black Hole army only in those two |
| `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_boot_m` | every mission boots through its CO screens (the armies, the map's size, units at full HP / ammo / fuel) |
| `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_cpu_m -j 5` | the computer's armies act: with the player passive for five days every enemy army moves (a third of its units by day 3, less where a garrison holds by design) and builds |
| `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_cos -k es_klaus -k es_livia` | the five COs in game: rows, texts, stars, unit cost, in a battle of the campaign; Klaus's Legio Aeterna heals 3 HP, Livia's Tribute collects 200 a property; Teams list only with `TANGOAW2_ES_VERSUS` |
| `python3 tools/estext/render.py` / `aw2dev campaign dialogue` | the dialogue as one readable Markdown file for review |
Tests: `aw2dev test python` (`aw2.esmaplib.test_esmaplib`, `aw2.eslib.test_eslib`), `cargo test --lib es_` (`es_co` numbers and texts, `es_text` parsing, `es_campaign`: roster, flags, unit caps).

Known harness limit: the Versus-based tests of the older kit (`ctx.start`, e.g. `crumb_is_pickable_in_versus`) fail on this branch with "map has 5 armies" (the harness's Design Maps navigation; the same on the base commit): the Emerald Sun tests use the campaign's own battles instead.

## The COs (what is built, what is not)
Numbers (firepower, defence, move, range) are `es_co::bonus`, read by `co_roster::stat`; luck, stars and unit cost are in the CO's table row. Klaus's Legio Aeterna heals 3 HP on every unit (a Thumb function
in free ROM, `es_co::heal3`); Livia's Ledger (+25% income) hooks `co_powers::income`, her Tribute and Mare Nostrum collect funds when the power is paid (`es_co::power_paid`, called from the one PayForPower trap
in `crumb.rs`); Varro's Sol Invictus heals 2. **Not built** (the design's rules that need new engine hooks; the numbers keep the powers strong): Klaus's COP "counterattacks against him do 25% less", Aurelia's Onager
splash (1 HP to units beside a target), Cassius's Triumph "move again after attacking". Pictures are placeholders (Dual Strike donors); the portrait work is the branch `escampaign`.
The five are listed in Versus only with `TANGOAW2_ES_VERSUS` (a development aid).

## Saves
The record is Flash slot 11 ("AWEC"). A mission saved halfway shares the BH Campaign's slot 12: the saved mission byte carries the campaign in its top three bits (`suspend::source_tag`; the DS and BH Campaigns keep 0, so their old saves stay valid), `saved_mission_of` / `drop_mid_of` only see their own campaign's save, so one slot serves both (a saved BH mission is replaced by an ES one and the other way round). That keeps the Flash at fourteen of its sixteen sectors with every slot in use; see docs/AW2.md "Saves" before adding another slot.
