# The Emerald Sun Campaign: building and checking it (`aw2dev maps gen`, `aw2dev campaign sheets | sim | difficulty | check`)

Sixteen missions played as Emerald Sun, the second custom campaign on the BH Campaign's engine. The story and the approved design are `docs/ES_CAMPAIGN.md`; this file is how it is built
and how every part is checked. Skill: `aw2-es-campaign`. Status: playable behind the development flag `TANGOAW2_ES_CAMPAIGN` (the Campaign sub-menu still says "Coming soon"
in a normal build; the flag turns it into a source of `campaign_model::SOURCES`); nothing is pushed or released until the user approves.

## Rules the campaign keeps (the user's)
- One battlefield a mission: **no two-front engine**. Tag missions (pairs) are fine (M8, M12, M13, M15).
- **Black Hole's inventions only in the two missions against Black Hole** (M9 Grain Ships, M13 The Free Port): the map file's `blackhole` line tags them; rule **ES01** of `aw2dev maps lint --source es`
  fails an invention tile anywhere else and a third tagged map; `aw2dev campaign check` repeats it. M16's toughness comes from two allied armies (Eagle and Hachi) with real production, funds, unit quality, reserve waves and CO powers; there are no inventions in it.
- **M16 is the hardest mission by a clear margin** (`aw2dev campaign difficulty --require-final`); M14 and M15 ramp up to it.
- **An army stops at 50 units** (AW2: its production and a trigger's reinforcements stop there; the sim saw Varro, started at 50, build and receive nothing): no deployment above 40 (the Rust test `no_army_starts_with_more_than_40_units`, the python test, `campaign check`).
- **The player is Emerald Sun in every mission**: its skin sits on army 1's colour, always one of AW2's four (Orange Star's mostly, Blue Moon's or Green Earth's where an enemy wears red) and never one an enemy or ally wears: a skin on colour 5 in a
  game that is not five-army once corrupted memory when the army bought a unit (found by the mission sim); the cause was the Dual Strike picture palettes' backup of colour 5 lying over the skin's table of figures in free ROM, fixed for every colour and army count (`aw2dev armyskin matrix`, `cargo test --lib rom_layout`), so the colour is now chosen for how it looks, not for safety (test `the_player_wears_a_colour_of_its_own`). Only one army wears it: Cassius's Second Legion (M4), Varro's Praetorians and
  the Accord's fleet wear the stand-in colours.
- Every unit deployed starts at full HP, ammo and fuel (`es_boot_m*` checks it); beaches are generous (RC04); every mission's story scenes have a song (`tools/estext/lint.py`, `campaign scenes --campaign es`).

## The kit
| Command | What |
|---|---|
| `aw2dev maps gen [--map es01 ..] [--no-build] [--check]` | runs the specs (`five/es/spec/*.py`: `Canvas` paints terrain, roads, rivers, blobs, scatter, properties, units, notes), writes `five/es/esNN.txt`, builds `src/es_map_data.rs` (tile joins checked as for the BH maps). `--check` fails when a text map differs from its spec |
| `aw2dev maps lint --source es [--map esNN] [--warn]` | the BH Campaign's rules on the ES maps (+ ES01) and the design-quality rules DQ01..DQ11 and SP01 (a finding fails: docs/dev/map-lint.md); the picture is `devkit/work/maps/lint/es/esNN.png` |
| `python3 tools/estext/lint.py [act1..act4] [--counts]` | the dialogue linter: grammar, every box two lines of 176 px (AW2's font from your ROM), voice slips (Klaus and Livia never shout or contract, Varro never shouts, nobody says who HOBB is), songs known and present, every key asked for by the code |
| `aw2dev campaign scenes --campaign es` | every scene with its song and box count |
| `aw2dev campaign sheets [--mission N ..]` | **review sheets**: the game's own whole-map picture of every mission (fog OFF; a fog mission also fogged), "MISSION N - Title" burned in, a contact sheet per act (`devkit/work/campaign/es/sheets/`) |
| `aw2dev campaign sim --mission N .. [--player bot cpu] [--seeds K] [--days D] [-j J]` | the **mission sim**: the test player (`aw2test.bot`, pad inputs only) or the game's computer playing both sides, from a record with the earlier missions won; one row each (result, days, units left, funds); appended to `devkit/work/campaign/es/sim.jsonl` |
| `aw2dev campaign difficulty [--require-final]` | the **difficulty score**: static (enemy start + reinforcement value against the player's, funds and ten days' income, the share that holds, lanes of attack) blended with the sims' results (CPU-vs-CPU win rate, days the test player needs); M16 must lead by 10% |
| `aw2dev campaign check` | the static rules: 16 missions, inventions only in M9 and M13, unit caps, a Black Hole army only in those two |
| `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_boot_m` | every mission boots through its CO screens (the armies, the map's size, units at full HP / ammo / fuel) |
| `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_cpu_m -j 5` | the computer's armies act: with the player passive for five days every enemy army moves (a third of its units by day 3, less where a garrison holds by design) and builds |
| `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_cos -k es_klaus -k es_livia` | the five COs in game: rows, texts, stars, unit cost, in a battle of the campaign; Klaus's Legio Aeterna heals 3 HP, Livia's Tribute collects 200 a property; Teams list only with `TANGOAW2_ES_VERSUS` |
| `python3 tools/estext/render.py` / `aw2dev campaign dialogue` | the dialogue as one readable Markdown file for review |
Tests: `aw2dev test python` (`aw2.esmaplib.test_esmaplib`, `aw2.eslib.test_eslib`), `cargo test --lib es_` (`es_co` numbers and texts, `es_text` parsing, `es_campaign`: roster, flags, unit caps).

Harness note: offline Versus lists maps by player count and shows the game's own tabs (Design Maps among them) only after L and R together, so `Game.boot_to_teams` presses `L+R` first (before that the Versus-based tests of the older kit failed with "map has 5 armies"). The view L+R toggles is a byte of the console's own memory (`online_maps::OFF_VIEW`, `0x0203DB0E`; it was a process-wide static that two peers and a straight replay in one process (`aw2_netplay_script`) flipped for each other, which failed every `netplay_powers_*` test): nothing the simulation or a menu reads may live outside the console.

## The COs (what is built, what is not)
Numbers (firepower, defence, move, range) are `es_co::bonus`, read by `co_roster::stat`; luck, stars and unit cost are in the CO's table row. Klaus's Legio Aeterna heals 3 HP on every unit (a Thumb function
in free ROM, `es_co::heal3`); Livia's Ledger (+25% income) hooks `co_powers::income`, her Tribute and Mare Nostrum collect funds when the power is paid (`es_co::power_paid`, called from the one PayForPower trap
in `crumb.rs`); Varro's Sol Invictus heals 2. The three rules the numbers cannot say are built as engine hooks (`es_co.rs`, "The powers' rules"; tests `tools/aw2test/tests/test_es_powers.py`): **Testudo**: a counterattack against one of Klaus's attacks deals 25% less
(`sub_08024DDC`'s entry trap cuts the counter's damage number and forecast to 75% when the battle's defender record strikes the attacker record of Klaus's army in his CO Power); **Onager**: an indirect hit
from Aurelia's army in her Super Power costs every enemy unit orthogonally next to the target 1 HP (10 points, a unit on its last HP stays; the trap on the battle's meter charge, `co_powers::attacker_charged`
-> `es_co::onager`; the attacker's cell is the move's destination `0x03003100`); **Triumph**: a human-played Cassius's vehicle that attacks in his Super Power is left not done by `sub_080424FC`'s
`orrs` (trap `0x08042532`, the battle's return addresses only), its bit is set in `es_co::MOVED_AFTER_ATTACK` (`0x0203C980`, one bit a unit slot, +0x28 the army of the last tick, registered in `ram_map`), the two Fire rows'
show tests (`0x0802CA78`, `0x0802CB20`) hide Fire for such a unit, any ordinary end of its action clears the bit, and the bits are cleared when the turn changes hands. The computer's Cassius gets the extra move
too: AW2's unit queue takes the unit again in a later pass and moves it, and `sub_0805FD64`'s trap (`es_co::ai_executor`) turns an attack action of a unit with its bit set into a Wait where it goes, so it never fires twice. The bits end with the turn and with Cassius's Super Power (a Tag Power's Change swaps the partner in: `forget_moves`), and ride in a suspended game after the tag tail (`suspend::TAG_LEN`, `es_co::moved_state`). All three are pure functions of emulated memory (no statics, no host state), so both peers and any rollback agree (`netplay_triumph`).
Pictures are placeholders (Dual Strike donors); the portrait work is the branch `escampaign`. **Each of the five has a theme of its own** (`music/es/*.m4t`, compiled by `es_music.rs`, played with AW2's own instruments: `docs/dev/music-kit.md`, `aw2dev sound`);
it plays on the CO's turn and in a scene that says `@SONG co:klaus` (M1 and M2 pre: Klaus; M3 pre: Aurelia; M4 post, M5 pre, M11 map: Cassius; M6 pre: Livia; M5 map, M14 pre: Varro).
The five are listed in Versus only with `TANGOAW2_ES_VERSUS` (a development aid).

## Saves
The record is Flash slot 11 ("AWEC"). A mission saved halfway shares the BH Campaign's slot 12: the saved mission byte carries the campaign in its top three bits (`suspend::source_tag`; the DS and BH Campaigns keep 0, so their old saves stay valid), `saved_mission_of` / `drop_mid_of` only see their own campaign's save, so one slot serves both (a saved BH mission is replaced by an ES one and the other way round). That keeps the Flash at fourteen of its sixteen sectors with every slot in use; see docs/AW2.md "Saves" before adding another slot.

## Colours, the skin and the design rules (the rework)
- **Colours.** A mission in which the player picks a CO started with the game's own colours for that case (the player in Black Hole's colour 5, the others shuffled), so Black Hole drew in Orange Star's red and the
  skin landed on Black Hole's inventions (the user saw it on the M13 sheet). `es_campaign::sync_colours` (called from the tick before `army_skin::sync`) writes the mission's colour to every army's player block; after
  the skin is installed `reload_palettes` puts the army's rows on the screen. **Test `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_colours_m`** boots all 16 missions and checks: each army's colour byte is the mission's
  (parsed from `es_act*.rs`), the Emerald Sun skin is installed on the player's colour alone, each army's palette row is its colour's, and on the real screen the skin's colours are drawn and none of the player's
  nation's own. `aw2dev campaign check` ties the map files' colours to the missions, the inventions to a Black Hole enemy army, and prints the player's slot per mission.
- **The player's slot per mission** (Emerald Sun skin on): OS in M1-M3, M5-M7, M9-M13; BM in M4, M8, M14, M16 (and M15 after its rework); never GE or OS where an enemy wears it; never Black Hole's.
- **Map design rules** DQ01..DQ11 and SP01 (docs/dev/map-lint.md): winding rivers, irregular patches, no unit blocks, room to fight (density .17, frontage 1.25; aim .12 and .9). `esmaplib` has `meander`, `cluster` and `spread`.
- **M14 to M16** after the rework: M14 storms Varro's camp (his arc ends there), M15 breaks the Accord's strait fleet (Drake and Sensei), M16 Stormhold against Eagle and Hachi (`es16.py`, 44x28, no fog).
