# The Emerald Sun Campaign: building and checking it (`aw2dev maps gen`, `aw2dev campaign sheets | sim | difficulty | check`)

Sixteen missions played as Emerald Sun, the second custom campaign on the BH Campaign's engine. The story and the approved design are `docs/ES_CAMPAIGN.md`; this file is how it is built
and how every part is checked. Skill: `aw2-es-campaign`. Status: playable behind the development flag `TANGOAW2_ES_CAMPAIGN` (the Campaign sub-menu still says "Coming soon"
in a normal build; the flag turns it into a source of `campaign_model::SOURCES`); nothing is pushed or released until the user approves.

## Rules the campaign keeps (the user's)
- One battlefield a mission: **no two-front engine**. Tag missions (pairs) are fine (M8, M12, M13, M15).
- **Black Hole's inventions only in the two missions against Black Hole** (M9 Grain Ships, M13 The Free Port): the map file's `blackhole` line tags them; rule **ES01** of `aw2dev maps lint --source es`
  fails an invention tile anywhere else and a third tagged map; `aw2dev campaign check` repeats it. M16's toughness comes from two allied armies (Eagle and Hachi) with real production, funds, unit quality, reserve waves and CO powers; there are no inventions in it.
- **M16 is the hardest mission by a clear margin** (`aw2dev campaign difficulty --require-final`); M14 and M15 ramp up to it: the static scores run M13 62.0, M14 64.7, M15 66.5, M16 73.4 (M16 leads by 10% over M15; the sims' dynamic part is not in these numbers). M14 has the Consul's second line (Tanks on day 3, Md Tanks on day 6, 14000 funds) and M15 the Accord's reinforcements from the north sea (Drake's Cruiser on day 3 and Battleships on days 5 and 8, Sensei's Fighters, a Bomber and a Copter, 14000 / 12000 funds); M16 got Hachi's Neotanks (day 6) and Md Tanks (day 10), Eagle's second Fighter pair (day 8) and 13000 funds an ally. The test player (`aw2test.bot`) cannot yet win a naval mission (M15) or a siege of this size (M16), so there is no days-to-win figure for them; the CPU-vs-CPU sim (`campaign sim --player cpu`) reaches no winner in 15 days on M14 (M15 and M16 not run), and `difficulty` ignores a run with no winner (it used to score it as the hardest: M14 read 83).
- **An army stops at 50 units** (AW2: its production and a trigger's reinforcements stop there; the sim saw Varro, started at 50, build and receive nothing): no deployment above 40 (the Rust test `no_army_starts_with_more_than_40_units`, the python test, `campaign check`).
- **The player is Emerald Sun in every mission**: its skin sits on army 1's colour, always one of AW2's four (Orange Star's mostly, Blue Moon's or Green Earth's where an enemy wears red) and never one an enemy or ally wears: a skin on colour 5 in a
  game that is not five-army once corrupted memory when the army bought a unit (found by the mission sim); the cause was the Dual Strike picture palettes' backup of colour 5 lying over the skin's table of figures in free ROM, fixed for every colour and army count (`aw2dev armyskin matrix`, `cargo test --lib rom_layout`), so the colour is now chosen for how it looks, not for safety (test `the_player_wears_a_colour_of_its_own`). Only one army wears it: Cassius's Second Legion (M4), Varro's Praetorians and
  the Accord's fleet wear the stand-in colours.
- Every unit deployed starts at full HP, ammo and fuel (`es_boot_m*` checks it); beaches are generous (RC04); every mission's story scenes have a song (`tools/estext/lint.py`, `campaign scenes --campaign es`).

Tests: `aw2dev test python` (`aw2.esmaplib.test_esmaplib`, `aw2.eslib.test_eslib`), `cargo test --lib es_` (`es_co` numbers and texts, `es_text` parsing, `es_campaign`: roster, flags, unit caps).

Harness note: offline Versus lists maps by player count and shows the game's own tabs (Design Maps among them) only after L and R together, so `Game.boot_to_teams` presses `L+R` first (before that the Versus-based tests of the older kit failed with "map has 5 armies"). The view L+R toggles is a byte of the console's own memory (`online_maps::OFF_VIEW`, `0x0203DB0E`; it was a process-wide static that two peers and a straight replay in one process (`aw2_netplay_script`) flipped for each other, which failed every `netplay_powers_*` test): nothing the simulation or a menu reads may live outside the console.

## The COs (what is built, what is not)
Numbers (firepower, defence, move, range) are `es_co::bonus`, read by `co_roster::stat`; luck, stars and unit cost are in the CO's table row (the cost field is a change added to 100: Livia's +10% is 10, the others 0; it was once 100 and 110, which doubled every price, found by `aw2dev co verify`). Klaus's Legio Aeterna heals 3 HP on every unit (a Thumb function
in free ROM, `es_co::heal3`); Livia's Ledger (+25% of what a property pays, 250 at the default Funds rule: `es_co::income_bonus`) hooks `co_powers::income`, her Tribute and Mare Nostrum collect funds when the power is paid (`es_co::power_paid`, called from the one PayForPower trap
in `crumb.rs`); Varro's Sol Invictus heals 2. The three rules the numbers cannot say are built as engine hooks (`es_co.rs`, "The powers' rules"; tests `tools/aw2test/tests/test_es_powers.py`): **Testudo**: a counterattack against one of Klaus's attacks deals 25% less
(`sub_08024DDC`'s entry trap cuts the counter's damage number and forecast to 75% when the battle's defender record strikes the attacker record of Klaus's army in his CO Power); **Onager**: an indirect hit
from Aurelia's army in her Super Power costs every enemy unit orthogonally next to the target 1 HP (10 points, a unit on its last HP stays; the trap on the battle's meter charge, `co_powers::attacker_charged`
-> `es_co::onager`; the attacker's cell is the move's destination `0x03003100`); **Triumph**: a human-played Cassius's vehicle that attacks in his Super Power is left not done by `sub_080424FC`'s
`orrs` (trap `0x08042532`, the battle's return addresses only), its bit is set in `es_co::MOVED_AFTER_ATTACK` (`0x0203C980`, one bit a unit slot, +0x28 the army of the last tick, registered in `ram_map`), the two Fire rows'
show tests (`0x0802CA78`, `0x0802CB20`) hide Fire for such a unit, any ordinary end of its action clears the bit, and the bits are cleared when the turn changes hands. The computer's Cassius gets the extra move
too: AW2's unit queue takes the unit again in a later pass and moves it, and `sub_0805FD64`'s trap (`es_co::ai_executor`) turns an attack action of a unit with its bit set into a Wait where it goes, so it never fires twice. The bits end with the turn and with Cassius's Super Power (a Tag Power's Change swaps the partner in: `forget_moves`), and ride in a suspended game after the tag tail (`suspend::TAG_LEN`, `es_co::moved_state`). All three are pure functions of emulated memory (no statics, no host state), so both peers and any rollback agree (`netplay_triumph`).
Pictures are placeholders (Dual Strike donors); the portrait work is the branch `escampaign`. **Each of the five has a theme of its own** (`music/es/*.m4t`, compiled by `es_music.rs`, played with AW2's own instruments: `docs/dev/music-kit.md`, `aw2dev sound`);
it plays on the CO's turn and in a scene that says `@SONG co:klaus` (M1 and M2 pre: Klaus; M3 pre: Aurelia; M4 post, M5 pre, M11 map: Cassius; M6 pre: Livia; M5 map, M14 pre: Varro).
The five are listed in Versus only with `TANGOAW2_ES_VERSUS` (a development aid).

## The world map (Dual Strike's Omega Land)
The Emerald Sun Campaign shows **Dual Strike's overworld**, like the DS Campaign: its source has `art: WorldArt::OmegaLand` (`campaign_model::SOURCES`). The map picture is converted from the player's .nds at run time
(`ds_worldmap.rs`: 480 x 240, tile-fitted onto AW2's map layer), AW2's own world-map procs draw the flags, the cursor and the LEVEL panel on it, and the DS Campaign's song plays on it. What is Emerald Sun's:
- **The 16 flags** are `es_campaign::region` (map pixels on Omega Land) in story order: Act I the south and west of the central island (M1 Landfall at Cape Astra on its south coast, M2 the ridge, M3 the Wall, M4 the Mistvale),
  Act II the centre and the east coast and the isle (M5 Dust and Wheels, M6 Tribute, M7 Mare Nostrum on the small island, M8 Ironhold on the eastern continent's coast), Act III the eastern continent (M9 the south
  peninsula, M10 Comet Pass, M11 the treasure road, M12 the snowfield bastion), Act IV the Free Port (M13), the northern isle (M14 Castra Maxima), the straits (M15) and the plateau of **Stormhold** (M16, the last mission's
  marker style). **Aurea's home island** is the south-west island (off the nodes: the legion sails from it). Every flag is on land and the flags are apart: `aw2dev campaign check` reads the picture from the .nds and the
  flags from the Rust and fails a flag in the sea, outside the cursor's limits, closer than 16 px to another, or a mission not opened by the one before (`eslib/worldmap.py`).
- **States**, as AW2's procs draw them: not shown (a locked mission has no flag), the open flag (AW2's white pennant, the cursor on it with the LEVEL panel), the cleared flag (AW2's starred flag: Omega Land's picture has no
  parts to repaint in a nation's colour, so the DS port keeps the starred flag on a won mission's point, `ds_worldmap::flush_sprites`), the last mission's marker (blue pennant). The campaign has no secret or optional mission, so no
  other markers; DS's map has no route lines and none are drawn.
- **Progression** is the BH engine's: a win opens the next mission (the campaign is one chain: each `m.needs` is the mission before), the map comes back with the new flag appearing and the cursor moving to it, a cleared flag can be
  picked again (replay; the record does not change), and the record (slot 11) and Continue restore the same map. `ds_worldmap::install` takes a custom campaign's own flags on Omega Land (before it, the 28 Dual Strike points were used
  by index), and `point_at` takes the camera's limits from the campaign's art (a flag east of x = 432 needs Omega Land's camera, also on Continue).
- **Tests**: `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_world_map` (the map at the start, mid-campaign and before M16 stitched from real screenshots: `AW2TEST_REVIEW_DIR` gets `es_world_map_<stage>.png`; and replaying a cleared mission),
  `-k es_progression` (**all 16 steps**: enter each mission through its CO screens, win it (test aid), assert the cleared and open flags, the cursor on the next flag, the record in Flash (won mask, unlocked COs), a save and Continue after
  M4, M8, M12 and M16's neighbour, then the epilogue, the staff roll and Select Mode; pictures of the unlock moments `es_unlock_<n>_before / _after / _after2`).

## Saves
The record is Flash slot 11 ("AWEC"). A mission saved halfway shares the BH Campaign's slot 12: the saved mission byte carries the campaign in its top three bits (`suspend::source_tag`; the DS and BH Campaigns keep 0, so their old saves stay valid), `saved_mission_of` / `drop_mid_of` only see their own campaign's save, so one slot serves both (a saved BH mission is replaced by an ES one and the other way round). That keeps the Flash at fourteen of its sixteen sectors with every slot in use; see docs/AW2.md "Saves" before adding another slot.

## Colours, the skin and the design rules (the rework)
- **Colours.** A mission in which the player picks a CO started with the game's own colours for that case (the player in Black Hole's colour 5, the others shuffled), so Black Hole drew in Orange Star's red and the
  skin landed on Black Hole's inventions (the user saw it on the M13 sheet). `es_campaign::sync_colours` (called from the tick before `army_skin::sync`) writes the mission's colour to every army's player block; after
  the skin is installed `reload_palettes` puts the army's rows on the screen. **Test `python3 tools/aw2test/run.py -k es_colours_m`** boots all 16 missions and checks: each army's colour byte is the mission's
  (parsed from `es_act*.rs`), the Emerald Sun skin is installed on the player's colour alone, each army's palette row is its colour's, and on the real screen the skin's colours are drawn and none of the player's
  nation's own. `aw2dev campaign check` ties the map files' colours to the missions, the inventions to a Black Hole enemy army, and prints the player's slot per mission.
- **The player's slot per mission** (Emerald Sun skin on): OS in M1-M3, M5-M7, M9-M13; BM in M4, M8, M14, M16 (and M15 after its rework); never GE or OS where an enemy wears it; never Black Hole's.
- **Map design rules** DQ01..DQ11 and SP01 (docs/dev/map-lint.md): winding rivers, irregular patches, no unit blocks, room to fight (density .17, frontage 1.25; aim .12 and .9). `esmaplib` has `meander`, `cluster` and `spread`.
- **M14 to M16** after the rework: M14 storms Varro's camp (his arc ends there), M15 breaks the Accord's strait fleet (Drake and Sensei), M16 Stormhold against Eagle and Hachi (`es16.py`, 44x28, no fog).

## Looks, weather, terrain variety and the army line-ups (round 3)
- **Look per mission** (Dual Strike's tilesets; `m.look` in the mission, one number: 0 normal, 1 snow, 2 desert, 3 wasteland): **desert** M5 Dust and Wheels, M11 The Treasure Train; **snow** M10 The Quiet Pass, M12 Siege of Iron; **wasteland** (scorched, water drawn as lava) M9 Grain Ships and M13 The Free Port (both against Black Hole); every other mission normal. M14 to M16 are untouched (the `bugfix` branch retunes them).
- **Weather** (`m.weather`, fixed for the whole mission; the engine already had all four: `ds_weather.rs` snow and rain with fog, `sandstorm.rs` Dual Strike's sandstorm: every long gun loses one cell of range, never below 2, plus the grains and the palette): **rain** (fog) M3 The Wall and M8 Hammer and Anvil; **sandstorm** M5 and M11; **snow** M10 and M12. Test `es_weather_m3/5/8/9/10/11/12/13`: the weather bytes (rain 2, snow 1, mode 3 = fixed; sandstorm weather 0 with mode 3), rain brings fog, the screen moves; `aw2dev campaign sheets` writes `es_weather.png` from them. `campaign check` lists look/weather per mission, wants 2 desert missions, a sandstorm, a rain and two snow missions, and under a sandstorm the player must have direct-fire answers (6 or more, twice its long guns).
- **Terrain**: missile silos (neutral `M`, AW2's own terrain, not an invention; ES01 allows tile 0x180) in M3 (west approach), M5 (middle ruins), M6 (the north market toward the Guildhall), M7 (north atoll), M8 (causeway head), M12 (east approach); neutral airports on M7's two atolls (joined to the home islands by sand spits so infantry walk there; lint AR03); **pipelines** only as closed barriers with a seam that matters (PP03, PP04): M5 (the Comet Pipe, edge to edge, two road gates and a seam on the Crossways road), M9 (the HOBB pipeline walling off the freight yard, its seam the yard's only land gate), M12 (the Iron Wall: a ring of pipe round the bastion town, five gates and a seam on the south road); M11's and M13's stub pipes are gone; Black Hole's **Piperunner** on M9's pipeline beside its pipe factory; **no ports on lava**: M9's and M13's quays have factories and airfields; M11's west ports are airfields; M14's legion port faces the open south coast; **Mount Sol**, M16's volcano below Stormhold's west gate (`m.volcano`, as the BH Campaign's M11: from day 3, every day, 3 cells of the ring (19..26, 10..17) for 4 HP to any army); reefs in M7, M9 and M13; ruins as grey cities in the desert maps. `Canvas.desertify(keep, rocks, size, seed)` dresses a map for desert and wasteland (sparse scrub, rock outcrops).
- **Armies: every army its own colour and at most one HQ** (lint AR01/AR02 on every ES, BH and Versus map; `campaign check`; Rust test `no_two_armies_share_a_colour`). New line-ups (player slot always Emerald Sun): M6 player GE, Colin BM, **Hachi OS** (was Sasha); M7 player OS, Drake GE, **Sensei YC** (was Eagle); M8 player GE, Max OS, **Sasha BM** (was Jake); M10 player BM, Kanbei YC, **Sami OS** (was Sonja); M11 player OS, Lash YC, Flak GE (stand-in colours); M12 player GE, Olaf BM, **Andy OS** (was Grit). BH findings: `bh30` has two HQs for one army (known, listed in `rules.KNOWN`); Versus has none.
- M6 is a bigger market town (40x32, 73 properties, 54 neutral, the Mill Water canal with seven bridges): own **22** properties at once by day 16 (lose on day 17).
- **Map design** (Oct 2026 rework): every map follows `docs/dev/map-design.md` (AWBW's Hall of Fame measured; rules MD01..MD08 fail an Emerald Sun map): villages about three cells apart, a contested middle, forward neutral bases, rivers crossed every few cells; each spec's docstring is its design note. Then 12% fewer neutral cities on every map (the user's rule): `Canvas.thin_neutral_cities` removes the ones that add least (crowded, by a road), never the middle's or a mission's objective, never below MD01/MD02/MD06 or leaving a lonely property (DQ08); a city in a road's line gives its cell back to the road.
