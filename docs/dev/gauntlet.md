# The gauntlet engine (`crate::custom_campaign`: `MissionDef::hidden`, `sleepers`, `cut_off`)

A gauntlet is one battle against several armies that come one after another: only the first line is on the battlefield; each later line comes when the
lines before it fall. BH2's M31 The Last Accord uses it (Nell, then Olaf, Kanbei and Eagle; then the stage switch to Aria's Citadel, docs/dev/stages.md).
It is data on a mission, so a game mode of its own can reuse it: the Gauntlet mode ([gauntlet-mode.md](gauntlet-mode.md)) does, with a map's mission started from Select Mode.

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| `MissionDef::hidden: Vec<Hidden { army, hq, zone }>` | The hidden lines: off the battlefield while they sleep: their HQ cell is plain on the map (the map header's `hidden 2 3 4` lets the map have no HQ for them), no units, controller 0 (AW2 gives them no turn), funds 0 |
| `MissionDef::sleepers: Vec<(army, Vec<hq>)>` | When each line wakes: the HQs that must have fallen first. A sleeping army is never defeated (`DefeatArmy` skipped) and is counted alive by AW2's end-of-battle check (`sub_08028BAC`, trap at `0x08028C0A`), so destroying the line on the field does not end the battle |
| `Cond::LineFallen { x, y }` | A line has fallen: the player holds its HQ, or its army was destroyed (the HQ turned a neutral property). A plain cell (a hidden HQ not built yet) has not |
| `Action::Reveal(army)` | The line comes: its HQ is built on its cell (under a unit standing there, if any), every property of its zone becomes its own (towers included, and the player's: a base raided before it came is taken back; HQs stay whose they are), its record gets its HQ cell, property counts and income, AW2's `RecountArmyProperties` (`0x080219AC`) runs, the screen is redrawn (`wasteland::render_map`), and it plays (the computer). Put `Spawn` (its wave) and its funds in the same trigger |
| `MissionDef::cut_off: Vec<(army, hq)>` | The lines the player's HQ capture routes (army, HQ: every line but the last; see "The rout"). `cut_funds` still takes back a fallen line's income at its turn start (nothing is left of a routed one) |
| Its wave (`Action::Spawn`) | A unit whose landing cell is taken lands on the nearest free cell it can enter within 4 (`landing`; every mission's spawns) |
| **The rout rule** (permanent, every gauntlet map) | Taking a line's HQ routes its army at once: see "The rout" below. `cut_off` is its data: each entry (army, HQ) is a line that is routed when the player takes that HQ |
| A rout | When a line's last unit dies, `DefeatArmy` turns its HQ neutral and the next line's controller is set at once (`wake_after_rout`); the wake trigger then runs after the next action |

M31's wiring (`bh2_campaign::bh2_31`): `wake(hq, before, army, chest, wave, scene)` = `AfterAction` on all of `fell(..)`: the scene, `Reveal`, the war chest
(25,000 and the savings, `war_chest`), the wave. Tests: `bh2_m31_hidden_lines_are_off_the_battlefield` (absent, the turn goes Nell -> player, the reveal),
`bh2_m31_nell_routed_before_her_hq_falls` (a rout), `bh2_m31_gauntlet_suspend_and_resume` (byte for byte after each reveal), `bh2_m31_progression`,
`bh2_m31_sleeping_nations_earn_nothing`. Pictures of every wave: `aw2dev campaign playthrough bh2 31 --player bot --god --waves --advance 6` (docs/dev/playthrough.md).

## The rout (every gauntlet map, by default)

**Rule (owner's, permanent):** when the player captures an enemy line's HQ, that army is routed on the spot: all its units are destroyed and every property it owns turns neutral; then the next line
comes as before. It lives in the engine, so a map gets it from `MissionDef::cut_off` alone and nothing per map.

How: a held HQ's capture skips AW2's "defeat the old owner at the end of the turn" mark (`com_tower::held_hq_capture`, at `0x08042822`). For an HQ in `cut_off` (`custom_campaign::rout_army`: the
army of that entry; stage 1 only) the hook instead calls AW2's own `DefeatArmy(army, 2)` (`0x08028894`) with the return set to where a held capture goes on (`0x08042834`). That is the game's rout
(what an HQ capture does in Versus): every unit record of the army is destroyed with the explosions (cargo, Dual Strike kinds such as the Black Boat, Carrier, Stealth, hidden Stealth and dived Sub
too: the test plants all of them) and the history counts the HQ capture. AW2 would hand the army's properties to the captor; the rule is **neutral**, so the defeat proc's step is patched at two
places (`custom_campaign::rout_neutral`, `rout_keep_hq_city`; only for a `cut_off` army with code 2): its captor register is 0 (as for an army defeated by losing its units) and the line's HQ cell, record
entry and `gProperty` entry are the player's city before its handover loop runs. The units die one after another over a few seconds and the map is busy (no menu, no Save) until it is over, then the next
line's scene plays. All state is the game's own (EWRAM), so suspend/continue, replays and netplay are the same on both peers (`SIM_VERSION` 45).

- The captured HQ becomes the player's **city** (AW2's HQ-to-city conversion, kept the player's by `rout_keep_hq_city`): the player keeps exactly one HQ, and the converted cell is of city class, so nothing the computer does to it can end the player's army (only an HQ-class capture does). Tested (`rout.check_captured_hq`).
- The next line: the `Reveal` trigger fires after the same action (the HQ is the player's: `Cond::LineFallen`), and `wake_after_rout` (the `DefeatArmy` trap) makes the next controller the computer's, as for a
  rout by lost units; a sleeping army is never the one routed (`defeat_army` skips it).
- **The last line is not routed**: its HQ is not in `cut_off`. Its capture wins at once (the `Win` trigger) and its army must still stand for the closing scene (an army AW2 has routed has no face for a speaker).
  The same goes for the last line of a stage that is followed by a stage switch (Iron Crown's Rachel, M31's Eagle): not in `cut_off`, so not routed.
- A line that falls by losing all its units is unchanged (`DefeatArmy` code 1, its HQ turns neutral; `wake_after_rout`).
- **A new gauntlet map**: list every line but the last in `cut_off` (army, HQ) and in `held_hq` / `held_more`; the last line's HQ is `held_more` but not `cut_off`. Then the rout is on.
- Tests: `-k gauntlet_rout` (Cinder Sea: Drake, Sensei, then Hawke wins; Iron Crown: Grit and Jess) and `bh2_m31_hq_capture_routes_the_line`: the army holds planted DS-only, loaded, dived and hidden
  units (`aw2test/rout.py`) and after the capture has 0 units, an empty unit layer and 0 owned cells, the next line is in and the player keeps control.

## The Gauntlet mode (its maps)

The Select Mode wheel's GAUNTLET entry (the mode's screens: docs/dev/gauntlet-mode.md) plays maps tied to a fixed line-up of COs, as War Room challenges are: the player picks any one CO and
fights the map's COs one after another in one battle. Each map is a mission of a small custom campaign, `gauntlet_mode::def()` (campaign source `gauntlet_mode::SOURCE`), so it runs on the
same engine as M31 (AW2's event engine runs only in campaign mode). Cinder Sea is the gauntlet engine's first use off M31 (player army 1, four armies); Iron Crown is five-army (the player is army 5, as M31's) and has a second stage.

**Colours and stages.** The enemies' colours are fixed in each army's data. Iron Crown's stage one takes Orange Star, Blue Moon, Green Earth and Yellow Comet, stage two's Asuka takes Black Hole, so `Entry::free` (`gauntlet_mode::free_colours_staged`: a plain colour must be free in every stage, a skin needs a free colour to sit on in each stage) offers **Silver Nova only**, which sits on Black Hole's colour in stage one and moves to Orange Star's at the switch (`gauntlet::player_skin` reads the enemies of the stage the battle is on; the player's army is army 5 in stage one and army 1 in stage two: `gauntlet::keep_colour`). Its designs follow the CO as ever (the player's CO's nation's, in Silver Nova's palette).

**The selector's order (rule, permanent).** The selector lists the maps **by the number of enemy COs, fewest first** (a map's later stages count: Iron Crown's four lines and Asuka's stage are five, so it is last), ties in `def()`'s order
(the older, easier map first). It is done by sorting (`gauntlet_mode::order()`, `rank_of`; the map-table id the game's list shows a map by is `FIRST_ID + rank`, `gauntlet_ui::id_of`), so a new map lands in its place by being added
to `def()` and nothing else. A map's identity is its **mission index** in `def()` (never its place in the list): the record (`ds_campaign::RECORDS + 8 * mission`), the colour memory (`gauntlet_ui::LAST`), the harness (`Gauntlet.open(map=<mission>)` presses DOWN until the
cursor shows that mission) and the tests all key by it. Today's order: Dustwind Basin (2), Brass Canal (2), Cinder Sea (3), Granite Spine (3), Harvest Run (4), Chrome Meridian (4), Iron Crown (5). Tests: `the_selector_orders_the_maps_by_their_enemy_count_fewest_first` (cargo), `-k gauntlet_mode_selector`.

**A map of one army.** A map may line up one whole army's COs (Harvest Run: Orange Star's Andy, Max, Sami and Nell). The engine needs a different army **slot** for each line (the hidden lines, their HQs, funds and turns are per slot), but nothing needs the slots to be different colours: give every
line the same colour (the same palette rows, the same HQ look: AW2 draws an HQ by the CO's country and the building rows by colour) and keep the slots distinct. The rout, the captured-HQ city (`owner` is the slot), the CPU's turn order (the slot order), the selector's faces (the
CO bytes of the header, in the order they come) and the colour pick (`Entry::free` takes the enemies' colours from the armies, so the player is never offered the army's colour) work as on any map. The lint's AR01 (two armies, one colour) is waived for a gauntlet map whose
sharing armies are one enemy team and not the player's (`maplint.rules.armies`). The same goes for **the skin**: Silver Nova's three COs on Chrome Meridian are three slots on one colour with `m.skin = Some((army, 0))`; the skin takes that colour for the battle, and the Black Hole line next to it keeps its own.
**An enemy skin and the scenes:** `ds_campaign::mission_skin` (the skin a mission's army wears) is on while an army has the turn (`pvp::current_army` != 0), not only while the map's main loop runs: a battle scene clears that loop, and the skin used to come off for every battle animation (the corps' tanks fought in the host colour's own). The Gauntlet's own pick (`gauntlet::player_skin`) never had the problem. Test: the pictures `-k gauntlet_lines_pictures_battle_gauntlet_07`.
A five-army map's fifth army (the player's) is always written as Black Hole's colour in the mission (`compile`); the player's real colour is the pick (`gauntlet::keep_colour`), which may be any colour no enemy wears, so a five-army map's enemies may wear Black Hole's colour too.

**A multi-stage map** also needs: `StageDef` with `armies` (the player's `CoSpec::Keep` and the boss `Fixed`), `held` (the boss's HQ), `deploy` (the stage map's army-1 cells), `funds_cap`, a `Win` trigger on `Cond::LineFallen` of the boss's HQ, `intro`/`victory` and day scenes; the selector lists every stage's enemies after the first stage's (`Entry::stages`). The save and Continue come back on the saved map and stage (`suspend::applied_ds` -> `ds_campaign::continue_gauntlet_map`: the saved mission, its header or the stage's, and nothing else: a `sync_mission` there once wiped the restored latches and put the first stage's header over the stage's, so a resumed stage two could not be won); a harness test starts a map by pressing DOWN on the selector (`Gauntlet.open(map=..)`).

Tests of map 2: `cargo test -p tango-gamesupport-aw2 --lib gauntlet_mode`; harness `-k gauntlet_map2` (`tools/aw2test/tests/test_gauntlet_map2.py`: each fall reveals the next line with its wave and chest, the stage switch carries the survivors with funds capped and the skin moves, Asuka's HQ wins, suspend and resume after a reveal and on stage two, every line fits its box), the picture sheets `gauntlet_map2_sheet.png` (`-k gauntlet_map2_pictures`: day 1, each arrival's scene and the map with the new line, the switch scene, Asuka's Keep whole and close up, the victory) and `gauntlet_map2_battle_sheet.png` (`-k gauntlet_map2_battle`: the Silver Nova army's battle scenes on both stages).

How to add a map (the Gauntlet's map selector and colour pick list it from its mission alone: [gauntlet-mode.md](gauntlet-mode.md), "Adding a map"):
1. A spec per map, numbered like `tango-gamesupport-aw2/five/gauntlet/spec/gauntlet_01.py` (`Canvas(..., campaign="gauntlet")`; the later lines' HQ cells stay plain and their zones' properties neutral; header `hidden 3 4 ...`), then
   `aw2dev maps gen --campaign gauntlet` (writes `five/gauntlet/gauntlet_NN.txt` and `src/gauntlet_map_data.rs`) and `aw2dev maps lint --source gauntlet` (0 errors; the BH2 rules apply).
2. A mission function in `src/gauntlet_mode.rs` added to `def()`'s missions: armies (army 1 `CoSpec::Pick`, the enemies `Fixed` with **the colour the map fixes for each** and on one team: those colours are what the player is never offered), `held_hq` / `held_more` for every line but the last,
   `sleepers`, `cut_off`, `hidden` (HQ cell and zone), one `arrive(..)` trigger per later line (scene, reveal, war chest, wave), `intro` / `victory`.
3. Lines: each `Line` is one box (two lines of 176 px: `tools/bh2text/lint.py`'s `px`); the speakers are the map's COs (the player's CO is anyone).
4. Tests: `cargo test -p tango-gamesupport-aw2 --lib gauntlet` (the generic checks run over every map: the rout wiring, waves on ground inside their zones, the selector's order; add the map's text to `text_of`) and the harness tests `-k gauntlet`: add the map to `aw2test/gauntlet_maps.py`
   (mission, player's army, each line's HQ, wave size, war chest) and `tests/test_gauntlet_lines.py` (the map starts from the selector, each fall reveals the next line and routs the one that fell, the last HQ wins), `cpu_port_idle_gauntlet_<mission>` and `cpu_progress_gauntlet_<title>` pick it up from there.
5. A wave: `aw2dev maps wave gauntlet_NN --zone x0,y0,x1,y1 --kinds INFANTRY=4,TANK=3,... --name NAME_WAVE` proposes the cells in a loose formation on ground their kind can stand on (paste into `gauntlet_mode.rs`, look at it in `aw2dev maps lint --png`). The shared wiring is `wire(..)` / `arrival(..)` / `chest::<ARMY, BASE, RATE>` in `gauntlet_mode.rs`: a map is its armies, its `Wire` lines (army, HQ, zone), its waves and its scenes.
6. The five-army maps (four lines) start the player as army 5: `Action::Win` names the player's army as the winner (1, or 5 on a five-army map).
