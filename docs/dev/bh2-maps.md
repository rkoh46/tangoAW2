# The BH2 Campaign's maps: pipeline, rules, pictures (`aw2dev maps gen --campaign bh2`, `aw2dev bh2`)

The BH2 Campaign (docs/BH2_CAMPAIGN.md) plays Black Hole against the Allies on the AW1-style world. Its mission maps are built exactly as the Emerald Sun maps are: each map is a small
Python spec, the spec writes a text map, `five/bhmap.py` turns the text maps into Rust data. Nothing is registered in a menu yet: maps and pictures come first. No ROM content is stored;
the ROM is read at run time for tile choices and the lint.

## The owner's rules, and where each is enforced
- **Inventions** (Deathray, Black Cannons, minicannons, Lasers, Black Crystals, Black Obelisks, Black Factory) are Black Hole's alone: **IV01** (error) refuses one that stands nearer to another army's HQ than to Black Hole's.
  The Deathray goes at most once a map, at the top edge (IV01 checks the row); none in Act I (a test).
- **Rivers** run source to sea or lake with mouths: RV01..RV05 (errors), the mouths and bridges by `Canvas.river` / `bridge`; a river is never cut by a road without a bridge (RV04).
- **Terrain**: SE01..03, TJ01..03 and RN01 (proper coast, shoal and cliff transitions), GB01/02, UN01..04 (no unit on impossible terrain), RC01..04 (no unreachable land, a Lander has a beach), RD01..02, PP01..04.
- **Beaches**: AW2 draws a beach one cell wide and long runs only along north- and south-facing coasts (the learned tile pairs refuse a shoal on a shoal and a long vertical run: TJ01/TJ02). So
  the island maps face their beaches north and south and run them for 8 to 12 cells: `shore(m, 1, box, dirs="NS")`; a test measures the longest run. Ports keep an open sea cell beside them.
- **No pointless Anti-Air**: an army fields AA or Missiles only where its opponents have or can build air (M3: Javier's airports and Fighters; M4 front 1: Drake's airport; M5: the Allies' air; M1 and M2: none).
- **No decorative filler, room to fight**: MD01..MD08, DQ04/05/08, SP01, the same thresholds as Emerald Sun; roads lead to properties (`Canvas.place` keeps every property near a road).
- **Units**: at most 50 an army, one HQ an army (the held second HQ of M5 is a `Q`, as BH1's M30), deployed units start at full strength (the builder gives full ammo and fuel), every army its own colour.
- **Fog missions** (M1): `aw2dev bh2 pictures` writes the full map with fog OFF and the fogged view (vision approximated: each unit's range by Manhattan distance, owned properties one cell).
- **Sky front** (M4's second map): `nohq`, air only, no property, no airport, no B Copter (BH1's M15 rule), exempt from the design rules.

## The engine's map limits (custom campaign maps)
- **1288 cells** (`0x508`: AW2's map buffer) and **40 rows** at most; width follows (64 x 20, 46 x 28, 40 x 32 ...): `bh2_maps.rs` tests it, `ds_maps.rs` checks every Versus map against it. The largest rectangle is therefore 46 x 28 (or 64 x 20): a "very big" BH2 map is at most about 1.3 times an ordinary one, never 2-3 times. (A second front is a second map, but a two-front mission swaps fronts rather than joining them.)
- **50 units an army**: production and a trigger's reinforcements stop at 50 (found by the Emerald Sun sim); a start of 40 or less leaves room for waves, so waves land on cells only while slots are free.
- Four properties' owners per map, 4 armies plus the player's fifth in a five-army map; `Q` second HQs are held HQs (`held_hq`, `held_more`).

## The silhouette rule (the owner's: no more ovals)
Every BH2 map must have its own shape. `aw2dev bh2 silhouettes` (also part of `aw2dev bh2 check` and the python tests) compares each map's obstacle mask (water, shoal, reef, lava, mountains) on a 24 x 16 grid with every other map's (intersection over union, limit 0.62) and flags the rounded blob in the sea (sea on all four sides, one landmass, ellipse fit >= 0.78, solidity >= 0.88).
The maps the owner has approved (Act I's five, Act II) are grandfathered: listed, never failed. Measured: M1 and M2 pass; M4 and M6 overlap 0.64 (both approved); M7 and M9 are blobs by the numbers (approved).
Vocabulary for a new map: land edge to edge with no sea, or sea along one edge (a coast); a peninsula; an archipelago; a bay or fjord biting deep into the land; a diagonal coast or a wedge; a delta; a canyon or valley between ranges; an isthmus joining two landmasses; a lake in the middle; lava or wasteland bays; a land that hangs from one edge as a U round a bay. Helpers: `snap` (the land cell nearest a waypoint), `snap_coast` (a coast cell for a port), `fix_rungs` (a length-one road rung that no game tile draws).

## Designed, not generated (the quality pass)
Specs build maps with helpers in `esmaplib` that make them look designed: `route` (a road through waypoints that follows the land: cheapest path over plain / wood / mountain with noise, never beside another road, bridges only at chosen `(x, y, "=")`
waypoints with a straight two-cell approach nothing else joins), `thin_roads` (no 2 x 2 road blocks), `belt` (ranges and forest belts along a line with passes), `town` (properties clustered at junctions, ports and HQ districts), `neutralize` (properties
far from their HQ are neutral), `stamp` (inventions and the volcano with its one-cell mountain wall), `clear_fire_lines` (nobody starts in an invention's firing zone), `shore` (beaches on north- and south-facing coasts).
Islands have bays, capes and peninsulas with long flat runs where landings happen; roads leave stretches of plain and wood untouched.
- **Ports** (owner's standing rule): PT01 + **PT02** (error, bh2): every port touches open sea (`~`; shoal, reef and river do not count), the sea is not a pond (24+ cells) and the door has sea beyond it. `aw2dev maps ports --source bh2` lists every port (and is the bh2-aware table now).
- **Day-1 fire** (**IV02**, error): no unit of either army starts in a Laser's row and column, a minicannon's four cells, a Black Cannon's cone or the Deathray's columns (`maplint/zones.py`); the harness (`test_bh2_review`) checks every unit still at full HP after Deploy and the first turn start (M4 has a Laser in the middle of the map).
- **Looks and weather** (`bh2_campaign.rs` `m.look` / `m.weather` / `m.fog`): M1 normal + fog, M2 normal, M3 desert + sandstorm (with Mount Ember), M4 normal, M5 wasteland; `aw2dev bh2 check` lists them and warns when two consecutive missions look alike, and, with the game's pictures (`aw2dev maps boot --campaign bh2`), compares their rendered ground colours (RGB distance under 40 with the same weather and fog, or under 22 for missions two apart). The engine's Wasteland look is the same tan as Desert (BH1's M11 renders the same): weather, not the tileset, tells M3 and M5 apart. A wasteland map has no sea (its water is lava): no ports, no river.
- **M3's Black Factory** (`MissionDef::factory`, the smart spawner `bh_smart`) and **Mount Ember** (`VolcanoDef`, ring road round it): `tools/aw2test/tests/test_bh2_factory.py` (`-k bh2_m3_`) plays the mission with the test player, logs every spawn with the field's reason (`factory_spawn_log.txt`) and fails if the factory does not spawn or a pick breaks the rules (Anti-Air / air only against air, foot soldiers only to capture or block, indirects only with a safe backline, a varied mix, never a Piperunner or Oozium), and reads the eruptions from the hazard cells.

- **Flash slot budget.** Flash is 16 sectors; with every slot in use (AW2's profile and slots 2..9, the BH record 13, the BH / Emerald Sun / BH2 mission saved halfway 12, the DS pair 14 and 15, Emerald Sun's 11) 14 are held
  and a write needs two free (a new copy and a new profile). A BH2 record in its own sector makes 15: with every other slot in use a write would fail. The development build uses **slot 10** for its record and shares **slot 12** for a
  mission saved halfway (the saved mission byte's top bits tell the campaign, `suspend::source_tag`: source 3 = `0x60`). Before BH2 ships, its record must not take a sector of its own: fold it into the BH Campaign's slot 13
  payload (0x120 bytes, with room) or Emerald Sun's slot 11, tagged by campaign, as the saved missions share 12. Nothing in the dev build is written unless the variable is set.
